Order Matters

Consent has to happen before Jack touches any coaching recap footage. The naming rule and the three-tier close script have to exist before Jack packages anything. Josh's dashboard and consent form should exist before the first public recap goes out, not after. Don't let production get ahead of the decisions above it.

JJDecisions, then personal execution
Build (decisions only you can make)
1

Pick the consent list

Choose 3 coaching recaps to go public first. Get explicit consent from those specific students before anything else moves.

2

Name any unnamed framework before it goes out

No topic goes public without a real name first. Check this before handing anything to Jack.

3

Write the three-tier close script

One line naming the Skool community, the Mastermind, and the placement line, in that order, self-segmenting. Write it once, hand it to Jack to insert everywhere.

4

Decide the one real thing to declare on camera

The $350K/month goal, a content cadence, whatever's actually true right now. Has to be real when you say it.

Do (can't be delegated)
5

Record one unscripted long-form conversation this month

With Evan Brown or Jalen. Pick the specific training topic before you hit record, not a loose catch-up.

6

Reach out directly to the 3 students on the consent list

This has to come from you personally, not a team message.

7

Film the public commitment video

From decision 4 above.

8

Record one full-depth framework deep-dive

Ask Evan or Jalen which framework students report the biggest breakthroughs from, then teach it at full depth, no watering it down.

JoshSystems and infrastructure
1

Build a single cross-platform dashboard

Views and engagement from YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X in one place. Without this, there's no way to actually measure whether the multi-platform synergy effect is real for Sales.io.

2

Set up a repurposing pipeline

A defined path from raw recording to Jack's queue to tracked short-form cuts per platform. A shared drive with a clear naming convention is enough to start, doesn't need to be fancy yet.

3

Build a basic consent and release form

For using real student calls and results publicly. Needs to exist before recap #1 goes out, not after.

4

Set up the "mids" channel infrastructure

A second YouTube channel for short highlight clips, separate from the main one. Not step one, build this once the core pipeline is already running.

JackProduction and editing
1

Cut the unscripted conversation

Once JJ and Evan/Jalen record it, one long-form YouTube cut, minimal editing, don't over-produce it, plus short-form cuts for every other platform.

2

Cut the 3 consented coaching recaps

Screen-share the real numbers, keep it dense and raw rather than heavily produced, the substance is what carries it.

3

Own the miner role for now

Go through raw footage and find the actual hook, it's rarely at the start. Restructure clips around wherever it actually is.

4

Packaging and titles happen last

After the cut is finished, not before filming. This is a sequence change from however titles get picked today.

5

Insert the three-tier close in every outro

Once JJ writes it, standardized, not improvised per video.
