Pre-Call Video — Introduce Yourself Before the Call

A short selfie video every closer sends the moment a strategy session gets booked. She sees your face, hears your voice, and knows exactly what's about to happen — before she ever gets on Zoom with you.

30–45 seconds
Sent right after booking
Selfie-style, no production
Why This Matters

She's Never Met You Yet

Right now the gap between booking and the actual call is where trust either builds or leaks out. She booked with a stranger. If the first time she sees your face is when you hop on Zoom, you're starting the call from zero.

This video closes that gap. It's not a sales pitch — it's you, being a real person, putting a face to the name and telling her exactly what to expect. Same principle as the call itself: connection over pitch.

What It Does For Show Rate

A stranger is easy to no-show. Someone who already feels like she knows you isn't. This is one of the cheapest things you can do to protect your calendar.

The Script

Read This, Then Make It Yours

Full Script — Fill In The Blanks

"Hey girl, what's going on! It's [Your Name] from FitMama Coaching.

I saw you booked your strategy session and I wanted to hop on here real quick and put a face to the name before we actually talk.

I'll be the one on the call with you, and I just want you to know going in — this isn't some pushy sales thing. I'm going to ask you some real questions about where you're at, and if FitMama's the right fit, I'll walk you through exactly what that would look like. If it's not, I'll tell you that too.

Your session is [day] at [time].

I'm sending over a short video in a sec — it'll help you get so much more out of our time together, so make sure you watch it before we hop on.

I'll personally text you a reminder before it starts.

If anything comes up before then, you've got my number, just shoot me a text.

I'm really excited to talk with you, [Name]. Talk soon!"

Beat By Beat

Why Every Line Is There

1
"Hey girl, what's going on! It's [Your Name] from FitMama Coaching."
Warm, casual, human. She hears a real woman, not a company.
2
"I saw you booked your strategy session... wanted to put a face to the name."
Confirms she's not being ignored, and this isn't automated — a real person saw her booking.
3
"This isn't some pushy sales thing... if it's not the right fit, I'll tell you that too."
This is the exact frame you'll set again live at the top of the call (see the Sales Handbook's Frame Setter script). Hearing it twice — once here, once live — is what makes her believe it.
4
"Your session is [day] at [time]."
Confirms the booking landed correctly and reinforces the commitment.
5
"I'm sending a short video in a sec... watch it before we hop on."
Sets up the VSL homework message that follows — she now expects it and knows why it matters.
6
"I'll personally text you a reminder before it starts."
A personal promise lands differently than an automated reminder — and it's true, since the reminder sequence is already part of the flow.
7
"You've got my number, just shoot me a text."
Removes friction. A question she'd otherwise sit on gets answered instead of becoming a reason to no-show.
8
"I'm really excited to talk with you. Talk soon!"
Ends on energy, not logistics. Last thing she hears is that you're glad she's coming.
Filming It

Keep It Rough, Not Rehearsed

  • Front camera, phone, no set. Polished kills the effect — she should feel like a friend sent this, not a company.
  • Say her name out loud where the script says [Name] — record it fresh per lead, don't reuse a generic version.
  • Good light, quiet room. That's the only production bar. Nothing else needs to be perfect.
  • One take is fine. A little imperfect reads as more real, not less professional.
  • Keep it under 45 seconds. If you're running long, you're over-explaining — trim, don't add.
