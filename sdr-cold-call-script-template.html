Discovery
Question 1: Okay sweet — so tell me, where are you at now with your XYZ journey?
Expand their response with at least 2-3 follow-up questions (Why, what do you mean, why do you say that, etc).
Question 2: How long has X been an issue for?
Question 3: What have you tried in order to achieve XYZ?
Question 4: And with that, what was the disconnect that didn't allow those attempts to give you the result you were looking for?
Question 5: Okay, got it — and so with you having struggled with {PAIN} for the last {DURATION}, why reach out for help now?
Question 6: Okay, and what is the goal with starting or getting help with your own [offer/niche]?
Chunk down if they don't give a clear answer.
How much are you wanting to bring in with your offer?
Why that amount exactly?
Try not to go too deep here — that's for the closer to do. Good enough to get clarity.
Question 7: So one last thing here — we value transparency, so to make life easier before letting you talk to the team, we always want to make the point that this is not the cheapest program in the world.
