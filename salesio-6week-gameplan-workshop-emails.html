Sales.io · Guest to Client Workshop (Tactic 05)

6-Week Gameplan Workshop Emails

Three variations for inviting your own students to a live gameplan workshop — full invite, short version, and a spots-filling follow-up. Fill in [Day]/[Time] and the guest cap before sending.

3 variations
For students, not cold prospects
Reply-to-book, no landing page needed
Variation A

Full Invite

Matches the original structure closely — full breakdown, qualifying criteria, capacity cap. Use this as the first send.
Subject: [First Name], want a real 6-week gameplan?
Hey [First Name], This week I'm leading a 3-hour workshop to help our students get laser focused on their right next move, and build a real 6-Week Gameplan. Would you like to join us? Details below. JJ — Jason Fried said, "Long term planning is what you thought. Short term planning is what you think." That's why we're planning the next 6 weeks. Long enough to actually move your close rate, land the role, or add real commission. Short enough that you can't waste it. The trick is figuring out the one thing to focus on for the next 6 weeks, and what to ignore. So here's what we'll do: Review your last 6 weeks — real wins, real lessons, no sugarcoating. Run the Close Rate Diagnostic — find the one thing actually capping you right now. Use the Sales.io framework to pick the 1-2 things that move you forward fastest. Build that into a real 6-week gameplan, together, step by step. It's a student event on Zoom, camera on, and I can only fit [20] people. We're on at: [Day], [Time] EST [Day], [Time] PST So if you... Are actively taking calls, not brand new to sales. Want a real, customized gameplan for the next 6 weeks. Can commit the full 3 hours, camera on, no multitasking. Are coachable and ready to actually do the work. ...this will be great for you. If not, pass, and let someone else take one of the [20] spots. If that's you, reply and I'll send the Zoom link plus the 6-Week Gameplan Kit.
Variation B

Short Version

SPEAR-style condensed. Use for a second touch, or for a list that already knows the format from past workshops.
Subject: [First Name], 3-hr workshop this week?
Hey [First Name], Running a live 3-hour workshop this week to help you build a real 6-week gameplan. [20] spots, camera on, real work. Want in? Reply and I'll send the link. JJ
Variation C

Spots-Filling Follow-Up

Send 24-48hrs after Variation A to anyone who hasn't replied yet. Only use if spots are genuinely still open, don't fake the scarcity.
Subject: A few spots left
Hey [First Name], Still have a couple spots open for [Day]'s 6-Week Gameplan workshop. Hadn't heard from you, wanted to check before I fill the last ones. Still want in? Reply and I'll send the Zoom link. JJ
