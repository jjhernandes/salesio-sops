Before You Dial

State of readiness

Sharp as a tack. Industry expert. Enthusiastic as hell. The prospect filled out a form after seeing Brennan on Instagram — they already know who BudgetDog is. Your job is to sound like someone who lives inside personal finance and investing every day, not someone reading a script.
Call Begins

Opener

SDR question
"Hey (FirstName)?" (question tonality)
SDR script
"Hey (FirstName), this is (NAME) with BudgetDog's team. How are ya?"
SDR question
"You filled out a form recently to get some help with your finances — getting a budget and investing plan in place — after seeing our founder Brennan on Instagram?"
If yes
"Yes, I remember that." → "Cool, well is that still something you're looking to get help with?"
If no / unsure
"No, what is that?" → "Really — BudgetDog, the company that helps you build a financial plan and actually invest with a system, does that ring a bell?"
If they still can't place it or aren't interested, hop off the call and move on. Don't fight for a dead lead.
SDR script — framing the call
"What is this call? I won't take up much of your time — this is a quick 5–10 minute chat to see one, where you're at now with your finances, two, where you're at with hitting your money goals, and three, to see IF and how we could help. That make sense?"
If they hesitate
"No" → "No worries — what's changed your mind?"
Discovery

Six questions, in order

Q1
"Okay sweet — so tell me, where are you at now with your finances?"
If new to budgeting/investing: "What made you want to get a handle on this now?"
If already tracking something: "How long have you been trying to manage this on your own?"
Q2
"Have you tried any coaching or advisors before to help you get a handle on this?"
Q3
"And with that, what was the disconnect that didn't let that get you the results you were looking for?"
Q4
"Okay got it — so having struggled with {PAIN} for the last {DURATION}, why reach out for help now?"
This is the same "what changed recently" beat the closer will dig into again on the HOW call. Get a real answer here, don't let it slide — it's what makes the transition to booking feel earned instead of scripted.
Q5
"And what's the goal with getting your finances in order — building the plan, investing, all of it?"
Chunk down if the answer is vague:
"How much are you looking to have invested / saved / bringing in?"
"Why that number specifically?"
Don't go too deep here — that's the closer's job on the HOW call. You're just getting a clear, specific number and reason to hand off.
Q6
Price qualifier
SDR script
"So one last thing here — we value transparency, so before I connect you with the team: this isn't the cheapest program out there. But if it feels like the right fit, we'll work with you. Investment ranges between $7,500 and $14,000 depending on what you need — is that reachable?"
This number needs to match what the closer will actually quote. Don't round it down to make the call easier to book — a mismatch here is what creates a "bait and switch" feeling on the HOW call.
Pitch

High-level overview → transition to booking

SDR script (skip if prospect is in a hurry — go straight to booking)
"(NAME), I definitely think we can help you out. What I'll do now is break down what we do at a high level, then get you connected to a longer call where our head coach will break down exactly how we can help — that cool?"
Overview
Track it. Grow it. Protect it. BudgetDog gives you a system to know where every dollar goes, turn active income into automated investing, and protect what you build — the same process behind 24+ millionaires in the last few years.
If they ask what the longer call is for
"This call was more of an IF call — to see IF we could help. The next one is the HOW call — our head coach breaks down exactly how we'd build your plan. All cool?"
Book

Lock the time

"If I could get you on (day), would AM or PM work best?"
"How about (time)?"
"And what time zone are you in?"
"Can you confirm the spelling of your first and last name for me?"
"Is the number I called you on now the one you have on you at all times?"
"Can I get your cell?"
"And your email?"
Close Out

Set expectations, hand off

SDR script
"Great — so I'm going to do one thing for you, and I need you to do one thing for me. You'll get a text from our team with more info on what we do before you hop on that next call. And the one thing I ask: be on a computer, in a quiet spot, good audio, not driving. Can you accept the meeting invite we send to your email? Perfect. And if you have to cancel for any reason, just call me directly so we can give that spot to someone else."
Confirm the calendar invite gets accepted before you hang up if you can — an unconfirmed invite is the single biggest driver of no-shows on the HOW call.
