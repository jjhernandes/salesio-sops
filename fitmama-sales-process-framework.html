Step-by-Step Sales Process

The Sales.io call framework, filled in with FitMama's real offer, ICP, and objections. Same 12 stages every strategy session should move through.

Core Rule

Never pitch just to pitch. You only present once you've earned the right to present. If discovery is weak, the offer feels like a sales pitch instead of the obvious next step she's already arrived at.

1
Intro: Respect First, Likability Later
Goal

Establish control, confirm time, remove pressure before you ask a single question.

Script

"Hey [Name], so glad you made it. Before we dive in — let me tell you how I like to run these calls. I'm going to ask you some questions to understand where you are right now. If it looks like FitMama is the right fit for you, I'll walk you through exactly what we would do together. If it's not the right fit, I'll tell you that too. Sound good?"

Key Principle: Wait for a yes. Do not move forward until she confirms. Calm authority, not chasing rapport.

2
Current Reality
Goal

Understand her actual life — work, kids, culture, what she's tried — before saying anything about the program.

Questions
  • Walk me through what your health and fitness situation looks like right now — what have you been dealing with?
  • What have you tried before? What happened?
  • How long has this been going on?
  • What does a typical week look like for you — work, kids, meals, all of it?
  • What made you decide to finally reach out?

Key Principle: Listen more than you talk. Every detail she gives you is material for the close later.

3
Problem Awareness
Goal

Get past the surface problem — weight, energy — to the real one, which is almost always identity.

Questions
  • What would it mean for you personally — not just physically — if you finally got this handled?
  • What does it cost you when you are not feeling strong and confident in your body? At home? At work?

Key Principle: Watch for self-sabotage ("I always start and fall off"), all-or-nothing thinking, perfectionism, imposter syndrome, and scarcity language ("I should be spending this on my kids, not me"). These aren't objections to close around — name them gently. They're the exact reason she needs the program.

4
Solution Awareness
Goal

Understand what's made her distrust "solutions" before — most FitMama prospects have already been failed by a program that wasn't built for them.

Questions
  • What have other coaches or programs told you to do?
  • Did any of them actually understand your schedule, your family, your food?
  • What's made it hard to trust that another program would be different this time?

Key Principle: She isn't solution-averse — she's been solved for wrong before. Most clients came to us after a coach told them to stop eating rice.

5
Future Pace
Goal

Build the picture of what changes if this actually gets solved — for her, not just her body.

Questions
  • If this was fully handled — you felt strong, confident, in control of your body — what would be different for you day to day?
  • What would that let you show your kids?

Key Principle: Keep it natural, not cheesy. This should feel like her own realization, not a script.

6
Consequence / Cost of Inaction
Goal

Make the cost of staying the same real, without manufacturing drama.

Questions
  • If nothing changes in the next year, what does that look like?

Key Principle: Only go here if the emotion from Step 3 is already present. If discovery was strong, you won't need to push this hard.

7
Transition to Pitch
Goal

Summarize what she told you and earn the right to present.

Script

"Based on everything you shared — especially [specific thing she said] — here is what I think makes sense for you..."

Key Principle: The pitch should feel like the obvious next step she's already arrived at, not a presentation you're forcing.

8
Pitch
Goal

Present FitMama tied directly to what she said — not a feature dump.

Structure
  • How the 6-month VIP 1:1 program works, day to day
  • The specific elements that match her pain — cultural foods (rice, adobo, sinigang) kept, not eliminated; time management around career, kids, husband; mindset and identity work; medical support for conditions like prediabetes, PCOS, postpartum
  • Who coaches her and how matching works (70%+ Filipino coaching staff)
  • What the community actually feels like — 97% Filipino, 95% moms, annual retreat
  • The 6-month timeline to change how she lives in her body
Check-ins

"Does that make sense?" · "Does this make sense?"

Key Principle: She should be nodding before you ever say a price. Every part connects to something she already told you.

9
Confirm Fit
Goal

Get her to agree, out loud, before price.

Script

"Before I walk you through what it looks like to get started — based on everything we talked about, does this feel like what you have been looking for?"

Key Principle: If she hesitates, go back to Step 3. Don't push forward on a weak yes.

10
Price Reveal
Goal

Present the right tier, calmly. No over-explaining.

Flagship (default)$5,000 · 6 months
Annual (pitch first if 30+ lbs, health complexity, or wants longer commitment)$8,000–$9,000 · 12 months
Spouse in the House (husband on the call)$7,500 / $12,500–$13,500
Script

"For where you are right now, I would recommend starting with our 6-month program. The investment is $5,000, paid in full, and here is exactly what that includes..."

Firm standard: paid in full upfront, no payment plans. Downsell annual to flagship if she hesitates.

Key Principle: Say it calmly. Pause after. Do not fill the silence.

11
Objection Handling
Goal

Don't argue. Get curious about what's underneath.

"I need to think about it."
"Of course — what specifically do you want to think through? Let's think through it right now together."
"It's a lot of money."
"I hear you — $5,000 is a real investment. Can I ask — what have you spent over the last year or two on programs, supplements, or things that did not work? And what has staying stuck actually cost you in energy, confidence, and how you feel in front of your family?"
"I need to check with my husband."
First read the dynamic: "Is he generally supportive when it comes to you investing in your health?" If supportive and available, invite him on: "Would he be able to hop on for 10 minutes?" If he joins → pivot to Spouse in the House.
"I've tried things before and they never work."
"That actually makes a lot of sense given what you described — none of those programs were built for someone like you. Let me walk you through exactly how we handle that."
"Is now the right time?"
"What would need to change for it to be the right time? Because in my experience, the circumstances do not change — what changes is the decision to make it work."

Key Principle: Objections are requests for clarity, not no's.

12
Close
Goal

Help her commit to the decision she's already arrived at.

Script

"So — based on everything we covered today, are you ready to get started?"

If yes
"Perfect. Let me get you the payment link right now and we will get you set up."
If maybe
"What would need to be true for this to be a yes?"
If no
"I appreciate you being honest with me. Can I ask — what is the main thing that is holding you back? I want to make sure I am not missing something important."

Key Principle: This is a one-call close. Do not leave with "think it over." Find out exactly what's in the way before you hang up.
