The DM Setting Flow

The general flow for every DM conversation. Four objectives, in order: her struggle, her goal, why now, then pitch the call. Know the objective, pick the questions that fit her, and follow the full conversation examples at the bottom.

Opener→ 1. Struggle→ 2. Goal→ 3. Why now→ 4. Pitch→ Book

DM rules: one question per message. Match her length (she sends one line, you send one line). Lowercase, warm, girls' girl energy. React to what she said before you ask the next thing. You're pitching the call, never the program, and never price. If she asks "is this gigi?", be honest: you're on gigi's team.

"Already doing ugc or just getting started?" is your best first question. It tells you which struggle questions to use.

Stage 1

Struggle

Objective: find the real thing stopping her from getting paid. Go deep on one answer instead of asking five different questions.

If she's just getting started

  • "how long have you been wanting to get into ugc?"
  • "what's been stopping you from starting so far?"
  • "do you have any sample videos or a portfolio yet, or starting from zero?"
  • "have you reached out to any brands yet? what happened?"
  • "how do you feel about being on camera?"

If she's already doing UGC

  • "how many paid deals have you landed so far?"
  • "are brands mostly paying you or just sending free product?"
  • "how are you finding brands right now?"
  • "what are you charging per video? how did you land on that?"
  • "do you charge extra when brands use your videos in their ads?"

Go deeper

  • "wait what do you mean by that?"
  • "how long has that been going on?"
  • "why do you think that's been happening?"
  • "how has that been making you feel?"

UGC struggles to listen for

She saysWhat's really going on
"i don't have enough followers"Biggest myth in UGC. Brands pay for the video to run on their page and ads, not her audience. Don't correct her in the DM. Note it, the closer uses it
"brands only send me free stuff"Stuck in gifted collabs. Doesn't know how to ask for money or what to charge
"nobody responds to my pitches"No portfolio, wrong brands, or a weak pitch
"i don't know what to charge"Pricing and usage rights (extra pay when brands run her video as an ad). Usually undercharging
"i'm awkward on camera"Confidence. Very common, very fixable. Lots of UGC is hands, voiceover, or product-only
"it seems so saturated"Fear she's too late. Usually means she hasn't started
"i got one deal then nothing"No system for consistent deals or monthly retainers
"i don't know what niche to pick"Overthinking. Stuck before starting
"i need better equipment first"An excuse to not start. A phone and good lighting is enough
Stage 2

Goal

Objective: get a real monthly number, then the life behind it. The number tells the closer what to build. The life is what makes her buy.

  • "if ugc was working exactly how you want, what would you be making a month?"
  • "are you looking for side income or to fully replace your job?"
  • "what would that money change for you?"
  • "what kind of brands would you love to work with?"
  • "what would your day look like if this was your full-time thing?"
Get to the why behind the number. Home with the kids, out of the 9-5, her own money, proving to herself she can. That's what she's actually buying. Log it word for word in GHL for the closer.
Stage 3

Why now

Objective: find out why this can't wait, and check she's serious enough to invest in herself. No urgency = no show and no buy.

  • "what made you want to finally make this happen now?"
  • "what happens if a year from now nothing's changed?"
  • "on a scale of 1-10, how serious are you about making this work?"

Commitment check (ask before you pitch)

  • "how much time could you realistically put into this each week?"
  • "have you ever invested in a course or coaching for yourself before?"
  • "if you found the right program, are you in a spot to invest in yourself right now?"
  • "is this a decision you'd make on your own or with your partner?"
Partner on the decision? Ask if they can hop on the call too. A partner who wasn't on the call is one of the top reasons for "i need to think about it."
Stage 4

Pitch the call

Objective: connect her struggle to her goal, and offer the call as the bridge. Use her exact words: what you said → what you want → the call gets you there.

  • "ok so from what you're telling me, [her struggle], and you want to get to [her number] so you can [her why]"
  • "honestly that's exactly what our team helps girls with. would you be open to hopping on a quick call with them?"
  • "they'll look at where you're at and map out exactly what you'd need to start landing paid deals"
  • "no pressure at all, if it's not a fit they'll tell you straight up"

Common pushback

Close it out

Book

Objective: lock a time. Give 2 specific times, don't just send a link and hope.

  • "amazing! i have [day] at [time] or [day] at [time], which works better? 💕"
  • "done ✅ you'll get a confirmation text. there's a quick video in there, watch it before the call so it's all about you"

Then log everything in GHL notes in her words: struggle, goal + why, why now, partner, investment readiness. The post-booking text and no-show protocol take it from there.

Don't book her if...

  • She's under 18
  • She's clear she can't invest anything right now and won't be able to soon
  • She's just looking for free tips and won't answer the questions
  • She has zero time to put into this

Stay kind, point her to Gigi's free content, and tag her nurture in GHL. A bad booking wastes a closer slot that a ready girl could have had.
