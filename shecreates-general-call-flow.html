The General Call Flow

One flow for every conversation, whether it's a setter dial or a closer call. Four objectives, in order: her struggle, her goal, why now, then the pitch. Not a word-for-word script. Know the objective, pick the questions that fit her.

Frame→ 1. Struggle→ 2. Goal→ 3. Why now→ 4. Pitch→ Close

The flow at a glance

StageYour objectiveMove on when you have...
FrameSet the tone and get permission to ask questionsShe knows how the call will go and said yes
1. StruggleFind what's actually stopping her from getting paidThe specific problem in her words, and how long it's been going on
2. GoalGet a real monthly number and the life behind itA number + why it matters to her personally

Setter vs closer. Setters run Frame through Why now, then pitch the call, never the program and never price. Closers run the whole thing, and go deeper on every stage. Same flow, same order.
Start here

Frame

Objective: calm authority. She should feel like she's talking to someone who's helped a lot of girls exactly like her.

  • "hey [name], so good to meet you! how's your day going?"
  • "you still have the full [time] with me?"
  • "so the way these usually go: i'll ask you a few questions about where you're at with ugc and where you want to be. if it looks like we can help, i'll show you exactly how. if not, i'll tell you straight up. sound good?"
  • "what made you want to book the call?"

Check the GHL notes before the call. If she shared anything in the post-booking text, use it: "you mentioned [her words], tell me more about that."

Stage 1

Struggle

Objective: find the real thing stopping her from getting paid. First figure out which bucket she's in, then go deep on one answer instead of asking ten different questions.

Sort her first

  • "are you already doing ugc, or just getting started?"
  • "have you made any videos yet, even just for practice?"
  • "have you been paid for a video yet?"

If she's just getting started

  • "how long have you been thinking about doing ugc?"
  • "what's stopped you from starting so far?"
  • "do you have a portfolio or any sample videos yet?"
  • "have you reached out to any brands? what happened?"
  • "how do you feel about being on camera?"
  • "what have you tried to figure this out on your own? youtube, tiktok, other courses?"

If she's already doing UGC

  • "how many paid deals have you landed so far?"
  • "are brands paying you, or mostly sending free product?"
  • "how are you finding brands right now? cold pitching, platforms, inbound?"
  • "what are you charging per video? how did you land on that number?"
  • "do you charge for usage rights or whitelisting, or is that included?"
  • "have you had any brand come back for more, like a monthly retainer?"
  • "what are you sending when you pitch a brand?"

Go deeper (the questions that actually matter)

  • "what do you mean by that?"
  • "how long has that been going on?"
  • "why do you think that's been happening?"
  • "how has that been making you feel?"
  • "what's that costing you? time, money, staying stuck at the 9-5?"

UGC struggles to listen for

These are the patterns that come up again and again in UGC. Know what's really behind each one so you know where to dig.

She saysWhat's really going onDig with
"i don't have enough followers"Biggest myth in UGC. Brands pay for the video to run on their page and ads, not her audience"where'd you hear you need followers?"
"brands only send me free stuff"Stuck in gifted collabs. Doesn't know how to ask for money or what to charge"how many free collabs have you done? what's that worth if they'd paid you?"
"nobody responds to my pitches"No portfolio, wrong brands, or a weak pitch"walk me through exactly what you send"
"i don't know what to charge"Pricing and usage rights. Usually undercharging by a lot"what did you charge last time? did that include ad rights?"
"i'm awkward on camera"Confidence. Very common, very fixable. Lots of UGC is hands, voiceover, or product-only"what do you think would make you feel confident?"
"it seems so saturated"Fear she's too late. Usually means she hasn't started"what makes you feel like it's too late?"
"i got one deal then nothing"No system for consistent deals or retainers"how did you get that one? what happened after?"
"i don't know what niche to pick"Overthinking. Stuck before starting"what products do you already use every day?"
"i need better equipment first"An excuse to not start. A phone and good lighting is enough"if you had the equipment tomorrow, what would you do first?"
Don't fix it yet. When she says "i need more followers," don't correct her in discovery. Note it. That belief becomes a powerful moment in the pitch ("you said you thought you needed followers, here's the truth...").
Stage 2

Goal

Objective: get a real monthly number, then the life behind the number. The number tells you what to build. The life is what she's actually buying.

  • "if ugc was working exactly how you want, what would you be making a month?"
  • "is this side income, or do you want it to replace your job?"
  • "what would that money change for you?"
  • "why is that important to you?"
  • "what would your day look like if this was your full-time thing?"
  • "how would you feel if you hit that?"

UGC-specific goal questions

  • "what kind of brands would you love to work with?"
  • "would you rather have a few brands on monthly retainers, or lots of one-off videos?"
  • "how many videos a month do you see yourself making?"

These make it real. "$5K a month" is abstract. "Three retainer brands I actually love, from home with my daughter" is something she can see.

Get to the why behind the number. Home with the kids, out of the 9-5, her own money, proving to herself she can. Repeat it back in her exact words. You'll use it in the pitch and the close.
Stage 3

Why now

Objective: find out why this can't wait, and confirm she's in a position to decide. No urgency = no buy.

  • "you've been thinking about this for [how long she said]. why now?"
  • "what happens if a year from now nothing's changed?"
  • "what's it been costing you to keep figuring this out alone?"
  • "on a scale of 1-10, how serious are you about making this work? why not lower?"

Commitment check (before you pitch)

  • "how much time could you realistically put into this each week?"
  • "have you ever invested in yourself before, like a course or coaching? how'd that go?"
  • "if this is the right fit, are you in a spot to start today?"
  • "is this a decision you'd make on your own, or with someone else?"
Partner involved? Find out now, not after price. If she needs her partner, get them on the call. A partner who wasn't on the call is the #1 source of "i need to think about it."
Stage 4

Pitch

Objective: connect her struggle to her goal, and show the Academy as the bridge. Only pitch what she told you she needs.

1. Recap in her words

"so let me make sure i got this right. you've been [her struggle] for [how long], and you want to get to [her number] so you can [her why]. and the reason it has to be now is [her why now]. did i miss anything?"

2. Get permission

"do you want me to show you how we'd get you there?"

3. Match the program to her struggle

Pick the 2-3 pieces that solve what she said. Skip the rest.

Her struggleWhat you show her
Doesn't know where to startThe step-by-step path from zero experience to first paid brand deal
Can't find brands / no one respondsLifetime priority placement on Kora UGC. Paid campaigns come to her instead of cold pitching. This is the biggest differentiator
Thinks she needs followers"you told me you thought you needed followers. you don't. brands pay for the video, not your audience. that's exactly how the girls in the academy get paid"
Awkward on camera / unsure if her content is goodCoaching and real feedback on her videos so she knows it's brand-ready
Undercharging / only gets giftedHow to price, charge for usage rights, and turn one-off deals into retainers
Tried figuring it out aloneCoaching + community instead of guessing with youtube videos

4. Check in before price

  • "how does that sound so far?"
  • "which part of that do you feel would help you the most?"

Her answer to the second question is her reason to buy. Say it back to her at the close.

Close it out

Close

Objective: give the price calmly, ask for the decision, and get payment done while you're still on the call.

  • "the academy is [price], one time. based on everything you shared, this is exactly the gap you said you want to close. let's get you started today."
  • Then stop talking. Let her answer.
  • Yes: send the payment link and stay on until it goes through.
  • Maybe: "what would need to be true for this to be a yes?"
Stay on until payment is done. BNPL failures at checkout are the team's biggest leak right now. If a BNPL attempt fails, try the next option right there on the call and log exactly where it broke in GHL.

"i'm scared i won't be good on camera""if you weren't a little nervous, that would surprise me more. that fear comes from not having a system yet, not from something wrong with you"
"i want to try on my own first""fair instinct. you told me you've been trying for [how long]. what would be different a few more weeks from now?"
"i need to think about it""totally get it. what specifically do you need to think through? let's do it together right now"
