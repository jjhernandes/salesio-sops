99%
My close rate this year. It's not a closing technique.
What You'd Assume

Better script. Better tonality. Better objection handling.

That's what everyone guesses. None of it's the real reason.
The Real Mechanism

A 99% close rate isn't won on the call.

It's decided before the call ever starts.
Reason 01

You can't lose a sale you never should have booked.

If the market's saturated, the margins are too thin, or the fit's wrong — I say so, and point them somewhere else. Every call left on my calendar is already a real fit.
Reason 02

The person who needs the sale the most always loses.

Neediness leaks. Prospects feel it before you say a word. Detachment from any single yes is what lets you close almost every one.
Reason 03

Belief dictates identity. Identity dictates action.

You're not selling a service. You're helping someone believe something different about themselves first. Once the belief shifts, the decision isn't really a decision anymore.
Reason 04

Composure closes. Conviction just talks.

50% of sales are lost in the first two minutes. Most reps lose the frame before they ever get to pitch. Silence isn't awkward. It's respect, and it's control.
Reason 05

Who you are off your calls is who you are on your calls.

You cannot perform composure you haven't actually built. The work that moves your close rate happens nowhere near a sales call.
The One Lesson

Stop trying to close better. Start disqualifying faster, and stop needing the yes.

Everything else in this deck is downstream of that one shift.
How To Actually Start
01
Disqualify out loud, early. Say the honest thing if it's not a fit — don't wait for the call to prove it.
02
Practice sitting in silence. Let a pause sit for a full three seconds before you speak. Feel how uncomfortable that actually is.
03
Do the identity work off the calls. Who you are at 9am on a random Tuesday is who's going to show up on the call at 3.

Everyone else teaches you what to say.
We teach you how to think.
