Option A · Mass Market, B2C
The Remote Closer Fast-Track
For people trying to escape a 9-to-5 through "making money online" who don't realize there's a faster door.
$7,500
Same core Mastermind, same financing as the flagship: 2-way split $3,750/mo, 3-way split $3,333/mo ($10K total), or Klarna/Affirm from ~$300/mo. Cold traffic converts on the guarantee, not a discount, price stays at parity with the flagship.

Instead, we place you directly into a paid remote sales role using our vetted job board and identity-based closer training, so you're earning commission in 30 to 60 days instead of building a business for years."
GuaranteeLand a paid sales role within your first month, or we keep working with you at no additional cost until you do. This is provable, not aspirational, it matches the actual track record.
DFY HybridActive application support into the job board's vetted companies, not full placement, but real hands-on help getting in front of the right roles. Bounded and scalable, doesn't turn into a staffing agency.

Existing Desire Channeled"Make money online / quit my job," already flowing, already searched for daily. This offer doesn't create that desire, it redirects it away from dropshipping and into something that actually delivers faster.

Option C — A Genuine Departure

Not something Sales.io currently does. Included because the assets to build it already exist, and it flips the business from selling training to individuals into selling staffing to companies, a different, likely larger economic model built on the exact same supply Sales.io already trains.

Option C · B2B, Big Brand Edition
The Closer Deployment Line
For established personal-brand businesses where a bad closer doesn't just cost a deal, it costs trust the brand spent years building.
$5,000 Proposed
Per closer placed, due after a 30-day performance validation window, ties directly to the guarantee. Plus an optional $1,000/month ongoing coaching retainer per placed closer. No existing pricing precedent for this line, these numbers need real testing against 1-2 actual clients before treating them as set.
What's Included
  • Vetting and identity-based training already completed before placement, not on-the-job training at the client's expense
  • A matched placement into the business, screened for fit with a personality-led, high-visibility sales environment
  • The 30-day performance guarantee, no payment due if the closer doesn't perform
  • Optional retainer: ongoing call review and quality assurance so performance doesn't degrade after placement, this is the recurring revenue layer, not required to use the placement service
Specific PersonEstablished coaches, course creators, and personal-brand-led companies at real scale, the category kings within their niche, who are still personally closing calls or whose internal closers are undertrained relative to the brand's audience expectations.
Specific ProblemGeneric recruiting agencies don't understand high-ticket, personality-driven sales, and at this scale, a single scripted or tone-deaf closer conversation can damage a brand that took years to build. In-house training pipelines take too long to stand up internally, and the founder ends up back on calls anyway.
Specific WaySales.io places closers already trained inside the exact ecosystem this business came from, Sales.io itself started inside Iman Gadzhi's ecosystem and has coached Hormozi and Iman-level teams, so the closers placed already understand how high-profile, personality-led brands actually sell.
"Most established coaches and course creators trying to scale their sales team hire from generic recruiting agencies or promote internally without a real training system. The problem is generic recruiters don't understand high-ticket, personality-driven sales, and a bad closer on a call with your audience can damage a brand it took years to build. Instead, we place closers already trained inside the same ecosystem we built our own company in, coaching Hormozi and Iman's own teams, so you're not the test case."
GuaranteePerformance-based: if the placed closer doesn't perform, you don't pay. At this tier, also frame explicitly as brand protection, not just a hiring guarantee, the real fear is reputational, not just a wasted lead.
DFY HybridBounded on purpose: Sales.io handles vetting, training, and placement, matched specifically for personality-led, high-visibility sales environments. The founder still manages day-to-day, this isn't full outsourced sales team management.
Proof to Lead WithSales.io's own origin and relationships, not a generic case study: launched inside Iman Gadzhi's ecosystem, coached Hormozi and Iman's own teams. This is insider credibility a generic recruiter can't claim.
Existing Desire Channeled"Protect what I've built while I scale it," the same desire driving every decision a category-king brand makes, this just applies it to sales hiring specifically.
💡
Why This Is High Potential

Sales.io already has both halves of a two-sided marketplace and is only monetizing one side. The training pipeline builds trained closers (supply). The job board already has relationships with vetted companies (demand). Option C monetizes the demand side directly, placement fees or a placement-plus-retainer model, which is a fundamentally different and typically higher-margin economic model than individual course sales. The big-brand framing also solves the classic problem of breaking into enterprise without a huge personal network, Sales.io already has the credibility asset most people spend years building, it just hasn't been used to sell placement yet. It also creates its own proof engine: companies that hire through this become case studies that make Options A and B convert better, and satisfied companies become a source of new job board listings, a flywheel that doesn't exist today.

Worth treating as a real test, not a certainty, this is unproven for Sales.io specifically, and the agency-owner segment of the current customer mix (10-15%) is a natural first signal of demand worth checking before building it out fully.

The Coach-Ambassador Program

Not a fourth offer, a distribution channel for Option A specifically. Turns your best proof into your acquisition engine, but gated hard so it doesn't pattern-match to the guru-affiliate structures Sales.io is positioned against.

Distribution Channel · Referral, Not Recruiting
Represent Sales.io as a Coach
Students who've actually made it become the people introducing the next student to the biz op, not just a link in a bio.
15% Proposed
Of the referred student's enrollment payment, staged 40% at enrollment / 60% at the referred student's real milestone (lands a role, or hits their first commission check). On the $7,500 core offer, that's roughly $450 at signup and $675 at milestone, about $1,125 total per successful referral.
What The Ambassador Gets
  • Coach title and standing within the community, not just a referral link
  • Featured visibility, case study placement, possible coaching touchpoint
  • Referral tracking so the staged payout is transparent and verifiable
Qualification BarVerified $30K+ earned since going through Sales.io, real income, not enrollment count or time-in-program. This is the gate that keeps this from becoming recruit-to-recruit.
The RoleQualifying students represent Sales.io as coaches, not affiliates with a link. They carry real title and visibility (featured case studies, a coaching touchpoint, community standing), and they introduce their own audience or network specifically into the biz op / intro-to-sales route (Option A).
Why $30K Is the Right BarIt filters for people whose success is substantial enough to be credible, not someone who barely finished the program. A coach who has genuinely won is a fundamentally different messenger than someone incentivized to talk anyone into signing up.
⚠
The Guardrail That Actually Matters

"Successful students earn commission for bringing in more students" is structurally identical to the affiliate models run by Jeremy Miner, Cole Gordon, and Andy Elliott, the exact positioning Sales.io is built against. The fix isn't avoiding this, it's staging the payout: partial on enrollment, the rest only when the referred student hits a real milestone (lands a role, hits their first commission month). That keeps the incentive aligned to "did this person actually succeed," not "did this person sign up," which is the entire difference between a coach representing real outcomes and a recruiter chasing headcount.

Proof to Lead WithThe ambassador themselves is the proof. This channel doesn't need a separate case study, the coach's own $30K+ result is the pitch.
