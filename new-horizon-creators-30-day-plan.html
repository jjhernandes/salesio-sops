Data Tracking

How it works today, then what to fix so it stays trustworthy under you.

How It Works Now

PieceDetail
DashboardCentral reporting tool (referred to on the call as "ShareYard" — confirm the actual name and who administers it, unclear from the recording). Tracks booked calls, shows, cancels, no-shows, close rate, show rate, cash collected, revenue generated, AOV, DPS, and DPL.
DPS vs. DPLDPS (dollars per show) is the sales-side number — reflects closer performance on calls that actually happened. DPL (dollars per lead) factors in show-up rate too, so it's the one that matters for setter performance and true profitability.
Source of truthPulled from the disposition ("dispo") form closers fill out after every call, cross-checked against actual payment processor data for anything cash-related.
Call contextManual notes on the GHL opportunity, an AI-generated lead summary on the contact card, and full searchable history in the #all-call-outcomes Slack channel by searching the prospect's name.
Known Weak Point

Show rate by setter is only as accurate as dispo form compliance. Tyler was explicit: there was a stretch where closers weren't filling dispo out consistently and the numbers went unreliable. It's reportedly clean now, but that's a rule that decays the moment nobody's watching it.

Don't Trust This One

The AI-generated call-outcome reports in Slack (built off the transcript) are explicitly unreliable at judging closability — Tyler's words, it'll call a closable lead dead or a dead lead closable. Always work from the manual notes, treat the AI report as a rough index at best.

6 Recommendations

  1. Pre-fill the dispo form from CRM/dialer data — name, email, phone, setter, closer. Ben already flagged this as the easiest fix available; right now closers have to hand-type all of it, which is exactly the kind of friction that causes the compliance gaps above.
  2. Make the Notion "Student Payment Summary" the single source of truth for payment status, and retire the older tracker Tyler showed once you've confirmed the new one is clean — don't run both in parallel.
  3. Add a same-day dispo compliance check — a simple daily report of which completed calls have no dispo filed yet — so accuracy doesn't quietly erode the way it already has once.
  4. Never use the AI call-outcome report as a closability signal. Make manual notes the actual record closers are expected to write, every time.
  5. Report DPS and DPL separately in whatever goes to the team — DPS for closer conversations, DPL for setter conversations and profitability. Blending them into one number hides which side of the funnel actually needs attention.
  6. Confirm what the reporting dashboard tool actually is and who owns/administers access before building anything new on top of it — the name wasn't clear on the call and this is the foundation everything else in this doc depends on.

How You Own It Going Forward

  1. Adopt the Notion "Student Payment Summary" as the single source of truth immediately — confirm it's clean, then stop cross-referencing the old tracker.
  2. Run the biweekly QC check yourself (or delegate and personally review it) — know what's past due before anything else.
  3. Sync directly with Renard and Samaria — they now own steady-state payment plans and failed-payment follow-up. Get explicit on the handoff: which failed payments are already theirs vs. still sitting with individual closers from the old structure.
  4. Decide who approves custom payment terms going forward — take it over directly or name a clear delegate, so it doesn't sit in an ambiguous middle the way it did during the transition.
  5. Push the dispo pre-fill fix (see Data Tracking, recommendation 1) — payment status data quality depends on the same input discipline as everything else.
  6. Watch failed-payment volume as a leading indicator. If it outpaces what Renard and Samaria can handle, that's the signal to add headcount before it turns into lost revenue.
