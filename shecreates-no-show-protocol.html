The No-Show Protocol

What to do the second a booked call doesn't show. Most no-shows aren't "not interested." She got busy, the kids needed her, or she got nervous. Our job is to make it easy to say yes again without making her feel bad.

Who owns it
Closer holds the Zoom, setter works the phone
From
Gigi's number (via GHL)
Window
7 days, then nurture

Who does what

One simple split: the closer stays on the Zoom, the setter works the phone. The closer never chases. The setter never pitches.

Closer Closer

  • Sends the "i'm here" text at +2 min
  • Tags the setter in Discord at +3 min
  • Stays on the Zoom until +10 min in case she joins
  • Marks No-Show in GHL + notes, then hands off
  • Goes to their next call. Done with this lead

Setter Setter

  • Dials her live at +3 min while the closer waits
  • Sends the reschedule text at +10 min
  • Owns every call and text for the next 7 days
  • Rebooks her and confirms the new time
  • Moves her to Nurture if she goes quiet

Part 1: The first 10 minutes

Closer and setter work this together. The closer holds the Zoom, the setter gets her on the phone.

Call time +2 min

The "i'm here" text Closer

hey {{contact.first_name}}! i'm on the zoom whenever you're ready 💕 [zoom link]
+3 min

Tag the setter Closer

Post in the SCA Internal Discord and stay on the Zoom. Don't call her yourself. If she joins while you're on the phone, you miss her.

@[setter] [her name] not on yet for my [time] call, can you dial her?
+3 to +8 min

Dial her live Setter

Double dial: call, let it ring out, call right back. A second call in a row reads as urgent and gets picked up way more. Your only goal is to get her on the Zoom. Don't sell, don't qualify.

She picks up

🎙 "hey {{first_name}}, it's [your name] from gigi's team! [closer] is on the zoom waiting for you right now, are you still able to hop on? i can text you the link again"

Can't make it now? Rebook her right there on the phone with 2 specific times. Then tell the closer in Discord.

No answer: voice memo

Hang up and send a short voice memo by text (15 seconds, smiling). A voice note gets way more replies than a voicemail.

🎤 "hey {{first_name}}, it's [your name] from gigi's team! [closer] is on the call waiting for you, no rush at all. hop on whenever you can, they'll hang out for a few more minutes"
+10 min

Leave and hand off Closer

Leave the Zoom. In GHL: move to No-Show, add tag no-show-1, and drop a note on anything you know from the post-booking text. Reply in the Discord thread "no-show, she's yours." You're done with this lead.

+10 min

The reschedule text Setter

no worries at all, life happens (especially for us girls juggling everything 😅)
here's a link to grab a new time that actually works for you: [reschedule link]
Tone is everything here. No guilt, no "we missed you" passive aggression, no "my time is valuable." She should feel relief, not shame. Shame is what makes people ghost for good.

Part 2: Getting her back Setter

From here it's all the setter. Every touch starts with a call. The text only goes out if she doesn't pick up. Closers stay out of it.

Same day, evening

Call, then the personal check-in

Call once. No answer? Send this.

hey {{contact.first_name}}! just checking in, all good today? 🫶
totally get it if something came up. you were so close to getting your plan for [what she shared, ex: landing your first paid brand deal] and i don't want you to lose that momentum
want me to grab you a new spot this week?

Use her own words from the post-booking text. That's what makes this land.

Day 1

Call, then give her 2 times

Call in the morning. No answer? Text this. Two specific options beat a link because she only has to reply "1" or "2."

hey girl! tried calling you. i have [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] open, which works better? 💕
Day 3

Call, then the value nudge

Call at a different time of day than before. No answer? Don't ask for anything. Just give her a reason to come back.

thought of you when i saw this 👇 [student win screenshot or Gigi clip that matches her goal]
she was in the exact spot you told us about. if you still want to chat, here's the link: [reschedule link]
Day 7

Last call + the breakup text

hey {{contact.first_name}}, i don't want to keep blowing up your phone so this is my last one!
if the timing isn't right, totally ok. if you still want help with [her goal], just reply "ready" and i'll get you on the calendar 💕

No reply = move to Nurture. She stays on the email list and webinar invites. Breakup texts get some of the highest reply rates in the whole sequence, so don't skip it.

When she rebooks: the setter books her, confirms the time in text, and calls her the day before. Then she goes back to the closer for the call.

Handling the replies Setter

She apologizes

omg i'm so sorry i totally forgot, my kid got sick
please don't apologize!! hope your little one is ok 🫶 want me to grab you a spot tomorrow or later this week?

"I'm not sure I'm ready / can afford it"

honestly i don't think i can afford a program right now
totally hear you. the call isn't a commitment to anything, it's just to map out a plan for you. a lot of our girls thought the same thing before their call. worst case you leave with clarity 💕

Don't quote price over text. Same rule as the post-booking play.

"Not interested anymore"

i think i'm good for now
no worries at all! can i ask what changed? just so i know for the team

Log her answer word for word in GHL. Then move to Nurture. Don't push past a clear no.

Second no-show rule

  • No-show twice = no auto rebook. Tag no-show-2.
  • Setter must get a real reply (not just a link click) before booking a third time.
  • Third booking must be a specific time she confirms in text, plus a reminder text 1 hour before.
  • No-shows a third time? Straight to Nurture. Closer calendar time is too valuable.

Prevent it before it happens

The best no-show protocol is fewer no-shows. Every booked call should get:

  1. The post-booking text within 5 minutes (see that play).
  2. The pre-call video link. Watched the video = way more likely to show.
  3. Day-before reminder that asks for a reply: "so excited for your call tomorrow at [time]! reply 'yes' so i know you're still good 💕"
  4. 1-hour reminder with the Zoom link.
The "reply yes" is the key. A reply is a micro-commitment. No reply to the day-before text = setter calls her that day to confirm. Don't wait to find out at call time.
