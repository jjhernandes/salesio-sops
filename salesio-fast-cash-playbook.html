7 Tactics, Two Audiences

Adapted from the BlackBelt "$10K in 10 Minutes" workbook set — same seven mechanics, rebuilt for Sales.io's two real audiences: closers working someone else's pipeline, and founders running their own list.

7 tactics
Closers/Reps + Founders, every tactic
No pricing, no fake urgency
How to use this: each tactic keeps the same underlying mechanic as the source but changes who's pulling the lever. A rep doesn't set price or own a list, a founder does — so the same idea shows up differently depending on which seat you're in. Pick one tactic, run it this week, don't try to run all seven at once.
Tactic 01
The 9 Word Email
Source: "9 Word Email" — short, personal, expecting a reply (SPEAR)
Closers / Reps
Stale Pipeline Revival
Not a list blast, a personal reopen. Pick your dead/stalled deals, one line each, same day.
Subject: [First Name]
Still figuring out [their stated problem]?
[Rep name]
Founders
List Reactivation
Already built and live this session — real Sales.io example.
Subject: [First Name]
Are you still looking for a higher-paying sales role?
JJ
Tactic 02
Price Rise
Source: "Price Rise" — a real price change as the reason to act now
Closers / Reps
The Deadline Push
You don't set the price, but a real upcoming company price change is a legitimate close-now trigger on stalled deals already in your pipeline.
"Hey [Name], heads up, the price moves to [X] on [date]. Wanted you to have first shot at the current rate before that happens. Want to lock it in this week?"
Founders
Announce & Anchor
Already built and live this session — the Content Academy swipe file, 4-email sequence.
"$2,500 is becoming $3,500. Here's why... Lock in the current price before [DATE] at midnight."
Tactic 03
Pop Up
Source: "Pop Up" — a surprise, short-window offer
Closers / Reps
The Surprise Slot
Real availability, not fake urgency. A genuine opening today, offered to one stalled prospect.
"Hey [Name], had a cancellation, I've got one slot open today at [time] if you want to jump on it."
Founders
The Pop-Up Cohort
A surprise, 48-hour intake window for a smaller/faster version of the program, sent only to your warm list. Gone when the window closes, no extension, no exceptions.
"Opening 10 spots for a 2-week fast-track, today only. Closes [time] tomorrow, then it's gone."
Tactic 04
50 Chat Starts
Source: "50 Chat Starts" — volume of personal, non-pitchy openers
Closers / Reps
50 Touches
Pick 50 stalled or old leads from your own pipeline. One personal line each, same day. Not a pitch, a check-in.
"Hey [Name], random thought, still on your radar to fix [X]?"
Founders
50 Chat Starts
Same mechanic against your Circle community or past-applicant list. Track replies, route them straight into the SPEAR qualifying sequence.
"Hey [Name], saw you in here a while back, still working on [their stated goal]?"
Tactic 05
Guest to Client Workshop
Source: "Guest to Client Workshop" — a live room does the converting
Closers / Reps
The Assist Call
Bring a peer or coach onto a stalled prospect's next call as a second voice. Two credible people breaks a stuck deal loose more often than one more solo follow-up.
"[Name], I want to bring my coach on for 10 minutes on our next call, he's helped a bunch of people in your exact spot."
Founders
Guest to Client Workshop
A live, invite-only session for your warmest non-buyers, people who applied, attended, or expressed interest but never converted. Teach one real thing, make the offer once, live, at the end.
"You applied a while back, didn't want you to miss this, live session Thursday, small group, real teaching."
Tactic 06
Looked, Didn't Buy
Source: "Looked, Didn't Buy" — remarketing to real near-misses
Closers / Reps
The Almost List
Every prospect who had a real call and didn't close goes on one list. One message, 2-4 weeks later, addressing the actual thing that stalled them.
"Hey [Name], has [their stated problem] gotten better or worse since we talked?"
Founders
Looked, Didn't Buy
Anyone who hit the offer page, booked a call, or attended the webinar and didn't buy gets one dedicated re-engagement email, naming the specific likely reason (price, timing, trust), not a generic nudge.
"You got close last time, want to guess what usually gets in the way at that point? Reply and I'll tell you straight."
Tactic 07
Would You Like To Join Us
Source: "Would You Like To Join Us" — low-pressure, genuine invite
Closers / Reps
The Referral Ask
For anyone who's seen real results working with you, past client, a friend who watched you close, a plain, low-pressure invite in.
"Hey [Name], you've seen how this actually works for me. Would you want in on it too?"
Founders
Would You Like To Join Us
A soft, no-pitch invite to your community/program, sent to warm people who've never been directly asked. Genuine, not a hard sell.
"You've been around a while, never actually asked, would you want to be part of this?"
