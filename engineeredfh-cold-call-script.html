Be in a State of Readiness

  • Sharp as a tack
  • Health & performance expert
  • Enthusiastic as hell

— — — — — Call Begins — — — — —

Hey (FirstName)? (question tonality)

Hey (FirstName), this is NAME with Engineered For Health. How are ya?

Where are you from? You filled out the quiz just recently about getting your bloodwork and performance actually optimized — from our page BLANK on Instagram?

*pause

Lead: Yes, I do remember — Cool, well is that still something you're looking to get help with?

Lead: No, what is that? — Really, the company that does lab-based performance coaching — deep biomarker analysis to actually find and fix what's capping your health and performance, does that ring a bell?

If they can't remember or aren't interested, hop off the call and move on

Ok cool.

What is the call? I won't be taking up much of your time — this is a quick 5–10 minute chat to see one, where you're at now, two, where you're at with your health and performance goals currently, and three, to see IF and how we could help you out. If that makes sense!

Lead: No — No worries, what's changed your mind?

— — — — — Discovery — — — — —

Question 1: Okay sweet, so tell me — where are you at now with your health and performance?

Expand their response with at least 2–3 follow-up questions (Why, what do you mean by that, why do you say that, etc.)

Question 2: How long has that been an issue for?

Question 3: What have you tried in order to actually fix it?

Question 4: And with that, what was the disconnect that didn't let those give you the result you were looking for?

Question 5: Okay got it — and so having dealt with {PAIN} for the last {DURATION}, why reach out for help now?

Question 6: Okay, and what's the goal with getting your bloodwork and performance actually optimized?

(Chunk down if they don't give a clear answer)

What would actually hitting that goal let you do that you can't do right now?

Why does that matter to you? (try not to go too deep here — that's for the closer, but good to get clarity)

Question 7: So one last thing here brother, we value transparency, so to make life easier, before letting you talk to the team, we always want to make the point that this is not the cheapest program in the world.

BUT, if you think it feels like the right fit, we will work with you.

Prices range between X and Y, is that reachable?

— — — — — Pitch — — — — —

High level overview: if the prospect is in a hurry or can't stay much longer, skip the overview and go straight to booking

So NAME, I definitely think we can help you out. What I'll do now is break down what we do in a high-level overview, then get you connected onto a longer HOW call which will break down everything deeper for you — is that all cool?

Overview…

Lead: What is the longer call for? — Basically we'll dive deeper into what you're currently doing with your health and performance, and then our head coaches will break down exactly how we can help. I like to say this call was more of an IF call — to see IF we could help — but the next one is more of a HOW call, all cool?

— — — — — Book — — — — —

If I'd be able to get you on X day, would AM or PM work best for you?

Sure, how about (time)?

And what time zone are you currently in?

Can you just confirm the spelling of your first and last name for me?

And is the phone number I called now the one you have on you at all times?

Can I get your cell?

And email?

— — — — — Close Out — — — — —

Great! So I'm going to do 1 thing for you, and I need you to do 1 thing for me.

You should be getting a text from our team with some more information, letting you know more of what we do before hopping onto that next call.

And the 1 thing I ask of you: please be on a computer in a quiet spot so we have great audio, and don't be driving or anything like that. And with that, can you also accept the meeting invite we sent over to your email? Perfect — also, by any chance you have to cancel, could you just ring me, just so we can make sure everyone gets a chance to hop on?
