A Straight Read on October

The good, and the part we're fixing.

05

October is only through the 25th and revenue is already $320,390, pacing for about $405k, in line with August and September. Booked calls hit 577, the most of any month, and pricing changed mid-month. With that much more volume, show rate dipped to 66% and close rate to 34%. That isn't a ceiling. It's the clearest opportunity in this report, and it's exactly what levers 1 and 2 are built to fix.

"Closer calls booked" is the sales tracker's scheduled count (355 in Oct 1–25, ~440 for the full month). It is lower than the 577 total booked calls on the main dashboard. *Today and August are the dashboard's actual cash per scheduled call; the $1M path figure is show × close × AOV only, so it leaves out installments and is conservative. The model: revenue = booked × show × close × AOV, plus ~$96k a month of other revenue (payment-plan installments, MinoAI and other sales outside closer calls). That ~$96k is the dashboard's $405,072 October pace minus what October's closer-call funnel accounts for (440 × 66% × 34% × $3,135 ≈ $309.5k).

AOV already grew from $1,907 in July to $3,135. Three of October's closers already average above $3,400. The Frontier $8k bundle, the ~$20k done-for-you offer and the 1-1 Accelerator push the mix up.
Capacity
6–7 closers

At full load to take 600 calls a month

Top closers carried 114–117 calls taken in August. At 85–110 calls each, 600 calls a month takes 6–7 closers at full load. That's why the bench lever matters.
The leads exist
~1 in 4

Qualified webinar leads we'd need to book

In one week (Oct 18–25): 18,989 visitors → 3,482 registered → 687 qualified for a call. That's ~2,975 a month, so 750 closer calls means booking about 1 in 4. Only 22% make $5k+/mo, so qualifying them is the work.
Why not just more ads
+$186k–327k

Extra ad spend a month if ads alone closed the gap

Today's ~$82k/mo spend runs at a 1.8–3.2 ROAS. Buying the whole ~$595k gap at that ROAS would cost $186k–327k more a month. Converting the leads we already pay for is the cheaper first move.
