October is only through the 25th and revenue is already $320,390, pacing for about $405k, in line with August and September. Booked calls hit 577, the most of any month, and pricing changed mid-month. With that much more volume, show rate dipped to 67% and close rate to 34%. That isn't a ceiling. It's the clearest opportunity in this report, and it's exactly what levers 1 and 2 are built to fix.
