10 Variations, One Per Situation

Each one solves a different problem: how warm the lead is, whether you have real proof to lean on, whether you're filling a specific number of spots or just opening a door. Pick by the "When to use" line, not by which one sounds best.

10 variations
Low-pressure → high-exclusivity
All sent as JJ
Low Commitment — Cold or Barely-Warm Leads
Group
01
Hey [First Name], I run a free community for reps who want to close more without sounding like a closer. Might be useful for you. Check it out here: [link] JJ
Use when
The lead is cold or you're not sure how warm they are. No real ask, just a door into the ecosystem. This is a first touch, not a close attempt.
Brainstorm
02
Hey [First Name], I'm getting a few reps together to brainstorm landing higher-ticket roles. Would you like to join us? JJ
Use when
Someone's engaged with your content but never taken action. "Brainstorm" feels like a peer session, not a sales call, so it's an easy yes for someone who's still on the fence about you personally.
Event-Based — A Real Date on the Calendar
Meetup
03
Hey [First Name], I'm running an event for reps closing $10K+/month in [location] in [month]. Would you like to join us? JJ
Use when
You actually have an in-person event booked (this maps directly to the quarterly Orlando masterminds). Only send to people who genuinely fit the stated tier ($10K+/month) — the specificity is what makes it credible, don't loosen it to fill seats.
8 Week Accelerator
04
Hey [First Name], We're starting a new 8-week accelerator to help reps land their next role fast. Would you like to join us? JJ
Use when
You're promoting a specific named, structured program with a defined length. Good for cold-to-warm conversion because the description does the selling, no exclusivity language needed here.
Pilot Program
05
Hey [First Name], I'm starting a pilot next month to help reps break into higher-ticket roles faster. Still working out the exact format. Interested? JJ
Use when
You're launching something genuinely new and untested. The "still working it out" framing lowers the stakes for both of you — good for early-adopter types who like being first, bad for anyone who needs certainty before committing.
Matched Outcome — Specific Spots, Specific Result
Working Together (Spots)
06
Hey [First Name], I'm looking for 5 reps who want to break into consistent $15K+ months. Sound like you? JJ
Use when
You want to fill a small, specific number of spots and want the recipient to self-identify with the outcome, not with you. Only use a real number, if you say 5 and take 12, the next one you send this to won't believe the next scarcity claim.
Working Together (Direct)
07
Hey [First Name], Want to work with me to go from where you're at to consistent $10K+ months? JJ
Use when
The simplest, most direct version of all ten. Best as a first-touch DM to someone already warm and qualified, not a cold list. No scarcity, no framing, just the ask.
Proof-Led — You Have a Real Result to Point To
Case Study
08
Hey [First Name], Just got a message from [Name] who went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Think you could get a similar result? JJ
Use when
You have a genuine, recent, specific win to reference — never a vague or old one. Strongest option for skeptical or previously-burned leads, since it leads with proof instead of a promise. Never fabricate or round up the numbers.
High Exclusivity — Your Best People Only
Private Group
09
Hey [First Name], I'm putting together a group for [year] where I'm working with a handful of top closers privately. Would you like to work with me? JJ
Use when
Recruiting your strongest, most successful students or alumni for something new and small. This is the most exclusive-sounding of the ten, only send it to people who'd genuinely recognize themselves as "top closers" — sent broadly, it reads as flattery, not truth.
Advanced Only
10
Hey [First Name], $10K+/month closers only — I'm running a class for our top students to go from $10K to $20K+ months over the next 12-24 months. Would you like to join? JJ
Use when
Targeting existing successful students or alumni for an advanced upsell, not new/cold leads. The gate in the subject line ("$10K+/month closers only") does the qualifying before they even open it — anyone who doesn't fit self-selects out immediately.
