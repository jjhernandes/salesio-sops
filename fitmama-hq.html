Call Framework Walkthrough
By Pop (FitPro CEO) / Will Nelson — the source for the opening & discovery structure below. Shared with Pop's explicit written OK for FitMama's internal closer onboarding. Do not re-share outside the team.
Adapted from Pop's licensed framework (see video above) for FitMama's call opening and discovery. This lives only here, behind the password — it's not in the public handbook.
1. Call Opening & Time Check
One-beat icebreaker (where's she's connecting from), then confirm time immediately — sessions run ~30 min depending on her questions, framed loosely for margin. No minimum time available right now? Reschedule, never rush it.
2. Story #1 — Where She Is & How She Got There (10–15 min)
What made her want this call today, whether it's new or long-running, what she's tried and why it stopped working. Drill into the pain — don't gloss over it. If she's had a coach before, ask how it went and what has to be true this time.
3. Story #2 — Where She Wants to Be & Why (5–10 min)
Project six months out — specific enough she can picture it (weight, look, feel). Ask what hitting that point unlocks for her — that's her real motivation. Reality-check the timeline if it fits the conversation.
Bonus — Quick Qualifying (Only If Not Done Pre-Call)
Should already be handled by the setter's DM flow — if not, ask about work (financial signal), marital support for the investment, kids, and if fit is still unclear, ask directly whether she's positioned to invest the right amount. Never framed as asking for a decision yet.
The 38-call library above covers founder and team calls, filterable by skill and buyer type — that's the primary place to study real calls now. Anything new outside of that goes to Khalil or JJ to fold in.
