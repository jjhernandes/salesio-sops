Context

What this session is

Who's on it
JJ's first 1-on-1 with Mariah, day one of mentorship.
Opening context
She raised her own income goal from $10K to $20K a month, isn't hitting it this month, and paid for mentorship specifically to close that gap.
Core lesson
Most of what feels like luck or fate is just unconscious pattern. Make it conscious and it becomes something you can repeat on purpose, not hope for.
Walkthrough

What was covered

01
Naming her baseline
Walked through JJ's levels-of-consciousness framework to find where Mariah actually operates from day to day, not where she wishes she did. Landed on Courage as her baseline, with real dips into Fear (imposter syndrome, "I'm just not that good") and Pride (stubbornness, needing to be right).
02
What operating from Love and Reason actually looks like
JJ's own operating level, and the composure it produces on calls and in life.
"I don't argue. I don't get upset. I don't get angry. It's always composed. Something about being calm, collected, and composed for people flips them on their head."
JJ Hernandez
03
The real trigger, named out loud
Mariah brought up checking her ex's Instagram story after seeing him with someone new. JJ reframed it as an unresolved loop, not a character flaw. the fix isn't more introspection, it's changing the behavior directly.
"If you're not doing the stalking stuff anymore, even if it bothers you, let it go, because that's going to control you. The amount of effort, time, and energy you're giving to that is going to take you away from you."
JJ Hernandez
04
Controlled luck
Mariah described herself as "a very lucky person." JJ named the actual mechanism: what she's calling luck is unconscious competence she hasn't made conscious yet. Turn it conscious and the outcome stops feeling like fate.
"You'll have controlled luck. There's never a moment where it won't work in your favor, because you know how it'll work in your favor."
JJ Hernandez
05
Her environment is already good — the leak is elsewhere
She doesn't go out, keeps a tight gym-and-calls routine, and has already cut most distractions. The actual leak: doomscrolling and FaceTiming friends, instead of the reading and studying that got her to her current level in the first place.
06
Time, attention, energy
JJ's own equation for performance: scattered time and attention produces low energy and willingness, even on a day that started well. When time and attention are in one place, decisions that used to feel confusing become obvious.
"Your time and attention, if it's all in one place, you'll make so much more money just doing that. It becomes so easy to navigate versus, what do you think about this? It's right in front of your face."
JJ Hernandez
