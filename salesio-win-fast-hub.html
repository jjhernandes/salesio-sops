Win Fast

Seven ways to put real money on the board this week. Watch the video, run the play, don't overthink it.

Structure adapted from BlackBelt's "$10K in 10 Minutes" workbook set — same seven mechanics, built for closers instead of coaches.
01 · The 9 Word Email
VIDEO GOES HERE
Stale Pipeline Revival
Not a list blast. A personal reopen on your own dead deals, one line each, same day.
Subject: [First Name] Still figuring out [their stated problem]? [Your name]
02 · Price Rise
VIDEO GOES HERE
The Deadline Push
You don't set the price, but a real upcoming price change is a legit close-now trigger on stalled deals.
"Hey [Name], heads up, the price moves to [X] on [date]. Wanted you to have first shot before that. Want to lock it in this week?"
03 · Pop Up
VIDEO GOES HERE
The Surprise Slot
Real availability, not fake urgency. A genuine opening today, offered to one stalled prospect.
"Hey [Name], had a cancellation, got one slot open today at [time] if you want it."
04 · 50 Chat Starts
VIDEO GOES HERE
50 Touches
Pick 50 stalled or old leads from your own pipeline. One personal line each, same day. Not a pitch, a check-in.
"Hey [Name], random thought, still on your radar to fix [X]?"
05 · Guest to Client
VIDEO GOES HERE
The Assist Call
Bring a peer or coach onto a stalled prospect's next call as a second voice. Breaks stuck deals loose.
"[Name], I want to bring my coach on for 10 minutes next call, he's helped people in your exact spot."
06 · Looked, Didn't Buy
VIDEO GOES HERE
The Almost List
Every prospect who had a real call and didn't close goes on one list. One message, 2-4 weeks later.
"Hey [Name], has [their stated problem] gotten better or worse since we talked?"
07 · Would You Like To Join Us
VIDEO GOES HERE
The Referral Ask
For anyone who's seen real results working with you, a plain, low-pressure invite in.
"Hey [Name], you've seen how this actually works for me. Would you want in on it too?"
