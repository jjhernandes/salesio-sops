Section 1

Foundations of Your Role

Company Mission

FitMama Coaching helps ambitious Filipina moms in their 30s and 40s get strong, lean, and confident — without giving up their cultural foods or their family.

We are not a generic fitness program. We are the only coaching company built specifically for Filipina moms, led by coaches who look like them, understand their culture, and know how to make results work inside their actual lives.

Why This Matters

Representation in health and fitness is broken. Most of our clients came to us after working with coaches who told them to stop eating rice. Who did not understand adobo, sinigang, family dinners, or the pressure of being a Filipina mom who carries the household.

We fix that. And that is why our community is 97% Filipino, 95% moms, and why women get results here when they could not get them anywhere else.

FitMama Sales Always Prioritizes

  • Fit over volume — we only enroll women we can actually win for
  • Connection over pitch — our clients buy because they feel understood, not because they were persuaded
  • Results over revenue — a woman who gets her life back brings three more women with her

You are not here to close deals. You are here to find the right women and help them say yes to something that will change their life.

Section 3

Ideal Client Profile

Who We Work With

She is a Filipina mom in her 30s or 40s. Career-driven and ambitious. She is often the primary decision maker and the breadwinner in her household. She has tried to get healthy before — but every program, every coach, every plan missed something fundamental about who she is and how she lives.

Primary Profile

  • Filipina (or Filipino background), 30s–40s
  • Mom — primary caregiver, likely managing kids, husband, and household
  • Career-driven — works full time, often in a demanding role
  • Primary financial decision maker — does not need to ask permission to invest
  • Has tried other programs or coaches and been let down
  • Ready to invest in herself — $5,000 is not the barrier; confidence in fit is

Fit Screen

Green Flags — Strong Fit
  • Specifically mentions wanting to keep eating her cultural foods
  • Has tried white coaches or mainstream programs and felt unseen
  • Has health issues that have not been addressed by standard fitness advice
  • Expresses urgency — a wedding, a retreat, a health scare, hitting a milestone birthday
  • Is the decision maker — no one else needs to approve the investment
  • Has been on the fence before but something made her finally reach out
Red Flags — Poor Fit
  • Unwilling to admit she needs to change
  • Plant-based or vegan — program is built around omnivorous cultural foods
  • Not willing to lift — resistance training is non-negotiable
  • Bipolar disorder or Schizophrenia — outside scope of what we can safely support
  • Severe Postpartum Depression — requires clinical care first
  • Pregnant — we do not coach during active pregnancy
  • TTC or actively in IVF within the next couple months
  • Looking for a quick fix, not a commitment to changing how she lives
  • No clear goal — vague, cannot articulate what she wants

⚠️ Do Not Enroll Poor Fits

These are not people we can win for, and a bad enrollment damages the community. If a red flag comes up during the DM or the call, do not push through it. Be honest with her. It is not the right time. Refer her appropriately if you can. A no now protects the community and her trust in us.

Common Mindsets You Will Encounter

  • Self-sabotage: "I always start and then fall off."
  • All-or-nothing: "If I mess up one meal, I quit."
  • Perfectionism: "I need to get my life in order first before I start."
  • Imposter syndrome: "I don't deserve to spend this on myself."
  • Scarcity: "I should be spending this money on my kids, not me."

These are not objections to close around. They are the exact reasons she needs the program. Name them gently. Help her see them.

Section 4 — Setter Role

Setter SOP — DM Qualification

Overview

Setters own the top of the funnel. Your job is to take leads coming in from paid ads, qualify them through DM, and get the right women booked on a strategy session with a closer.

Your job is not to pitch the program. Your job is to find out if she belongs in this community — and if she does, get her excited about the conversation.

DM Qualification Flow

1

Respond Fast

Respond to every new lead within 5 minutes when possible. Speed signals that this is a real, active community — not a funnel that goes cold.

2

Open With Curiosity, Not a Pitch

Start with a genuine question about where she is right now. Warm, real, no pressure. Wait for her to answer. Listen. Then respond to what she actually said — do not jump to your next script line.

Opener Options

"Hey [Name]! So glad you reached out. What made you decide to look into this right now?"

"Hey [Name]! What's been the biggest struggle for you lately when it comes to your health?"

3

Qualify the Fit

Before sending anyone to an application, confirm these four things. Screen for red flags here — if any show up, do not book the call. Handle it honestly in the DM.

  • Is she Filipino or does she connect with the cultural focus of the program?
  • Is she a mom with real time constraints — not just generally busy?
  • Does she have a clear goal or health issue she wants to address?
  • Is she the decision maker? (She can say yes without needing to check with a partner.)
4

Bridge to the Application

Application link: fitmamacoaching.com/apply-for-vip-coaching

Bridge

"Before I get you connected with our team, I want to make sure we can actually help you and set you up for the right program. We have a quick intake form — takes about 3 minutes. Can I send it over?"

5

Follow Up on the Application

If she has not submitted within 24 hours, follow up once. Do not follow up more than twice. If she does not engage, move her to Nurture in GoHighLevel.

Follow-Up

"Hey [Name], just wanted to make sure you got the link! The form only takes a few minutes and it helps us get ready for your call so we are not starting from scratch."

6

Book the Call

Once the application is submitted, book her on a strategy session within 24 hours.

  • Use the team booking link or GoHighLevel scheduling
  • Confirm the call via DM with date and time
  • Send the pre-call homework immediately after booking
7

Send Pre-Call Homework

Every booked prospect watches the VSL and completes the confirmation page before her call. This is not optional.

Homework Message

"You are all set for [day] at [time]! Before your call, I am sending you a short video from Khalil — watch it when you get a chance. It will make your strategy session way more valuable. [VSL link]"

8

Reminder Sequence

  • 24 hours before: confirm she watched the VSL and remind her of the time
  • 1 hour before: quick check-in message

⚠️ Book Rate Benchmark: 3%

3% of leads is the floor, not the ceiling. Track your own book rate weekly and bring it to your check-ins.

Section 6 — Closer Role

Sales Call Process

Overview

Every strategy session is a one-call close. The goal is not to give a great presentation. The goal is to understand her well enough that when you present the program, it feels like you built it for her — because in that moment, you did.

Our clients are emotionally intelligent women who have been sold to before by programs that did not deliver. They do not need pressure. They need to feel seen. The rep who listens the most wins.

Before the Call — Preparation

  • Read the setter's brief in GoHighLevel
  • Review her application answers — know her situation before she opens her mouth
  • Have all offer tiers ready: flagship $5K | annual $8–9K | Spouse in the House $7.5K (6mo) or $12.5K–$13.5K (annual)
  • Note whether the husband was flagged — be ready to pivot to Spouse in the House if he joins
  • Know the coaching availability: 149 spots remaining across the coaching team
  • Fathom recording enabled before the call starts

Looking for the detailed call-opening script?

The step-by-step opening and discovery framework (with full scripts) lives in the password-protected Call Vault, inside the Team HQ — it includes licensed material that stays internal-only. This page covers the FitMama-original structure and standards.

1

Set the Frame (2–3 min)

Take control of the call immediately. Remove pressure before you ask a single question. Wait for a yes. Do not move forward until she confirms.

Frame Setter

"Hey [Name], so glad you made it. Before we dive in — let me tell you how I like to run these calls. I'm going to ask you some questions to understand where you are right now. If it looks like FitMama is the right fit for you, I'll walk you through exactly what we would do together. If it's not the right fit, I'll tell you that too. Sound good?"

2

Investigate Her Current Reality (10–15 min)

Understand her life before you say anything about the program. Listen more than you talk — every detail she gives you is material for the close. Take notes in GoHighLevel as she speaks.

Discovery Questions

"Walk me through what your health and fitness situation looks like right now — what have you been dealing with?"

"What have you tried before? What happened?" / "How long has this been going on?"

"What does a typical week look like for you — work, kids, meals, all of it?"

"What made you decide to finally reach out?"

3

Find the Real Problem (5–10 min)

The surface problem is usually weight or energy. The real problem is almost always identity — how she sees herself, what she feels she deserves, what message she is sending her kids. You are looking for emotional weight, urgency, and something she can picture. The more specific she gets, the stronger the close.

Depth Questions

"What would it mean for you personally — not just physically — if you finally got this handled?"

"What does it cost you when you are not feeling strong and confident in your body? At home? At work?"

"If nothing changes in the next year, what does that look like?"

4

Present the Program (10–12 min)

Now — and only now — introduce FitMama. Tie every element directly to what she just told you. Keep it conversational. Stop and ask "Does this make sense?" after key points. She should be nodding before you ever mention price.

  • How the program works and what her experience will look like day to day
  • The specific elements that match her pain (cultural foods, time management, mindset work, medical support)
  • The coaching staff and how assignment works (matched by fit)
  • The community — give her a real picture of what it feels like to be inside it
  • The timeline: 6 months to fundamentally change how she lives in her body
Bridge

"Based on everything you shared — especially [specific thing she said] — here is what I think makes sense for you..."

5

Anchor the Value Before Price

If yes: move to the offer. If hesitant: go back to Step 3. Reestablish what is at stake before you present price. Do not skip this.

Anchor

"Before I walk you through what it looks like to get started — based on everything we talked about, does this feel like what you have been looking for?"

6

Present the Offer

Default to the flagship. Upsell to annual if she has significant health complexity or has expressed wanting a longer commitment. If the husband is on the call or she mentions him as a decision maker, pivot to Spouse in the House. Pause after presenting the investment. Let her respond. Do not fill the silence.

Default Offer

"For where you are right now, I would recommend starting with our 6-month program. The investment is $5,000, paid in full, and here is exactly what that includes..."

If Strong Candidate for Annual

"Actually — based on [her specific situation], I want to walk you through our annual program first. The reason is [specific reason tied to her]. Here is the difference..."

If Husband Is On the Call

"Since you both are here — I actually want to walk you through something a little different that was designed exactly for this situation..." (see Section 7)

7

Handle Objections and Close

See Section 7 for objection handling. Once objections are addressed:

Close

"So — based on everything we covered today, are you ready to get started?"

⚠️ This Is a One-Call Close

Do not leave the call with "think it over and let me know." If she cannot decide today, find out exactly what is in the way before you hang up.

Section 7

Objection Handling

How to Handle Objections

Objections are not nos. They are requests for more clarity or more confidence. Do not argue. Get curious about what is underneath the objection, then address the real concern.

💬 "I need to think about it."

"Of course — what specifically do you want to think through? Let's think through it right now together."

Most of the time, she does not need time. She needs reassurance on something specific. Find it.

💬 "It's a lot of money."

"I hear you — $5,000 is a real investment. Can I ask — what have you spent over the last year or two on programs, supplements, or things that did not work? And what has staying stuck actually cost you in energy, confidence, and how you feel in front of your family?"

Do not apologize for the price. Help her compare the right numbers.

💬 "I need to check with my husband."

First — get a read on the dynamic.

"Is he generally supportive when it comes to you investing in your health? What does he usually say when you bring things like this to him?"

If he is supportive and available: invite him onto the call right now.

"Would he be able to hop on for 10 minutes? I'd love to give him the full picture so you two can make the decision together — and honestly, there is an option that might make a lot of sense for both of you."

If he joins the call — pivot to Spouse in the House:

"Since you're both here — I want to walk you through something different. We have a program specifically designed for couples where the husband gets involved too. It's called Spouse in the House. Here is how it works and what the investment looks like..."

6-month Spouse in the House: $7,500 · Annual: $12,500–$13,500. When the household commits together, results are faster and the family dynamic shifts. This is the strongest close available when the husband is in the room.

💬 "I've tried things before and they never work."

"That actually makes a lot of sense given what you described — none of those programs were built for someone like you. The reason they did not work is the same reason this is different. They did not account for [cultural food / your schedule / your identity / your health situation]. Here — let me walk you through exactly how we handle that..."

💬 "Is now the right time?"

"What would need to change for it to be the right time? Because in my experience, the circumstances do not change — what changes is the decision to make it work."

💬 "What if I don't get results?"

"Khalil has a 100% success rate with his 1:1 clients. Every client who has stayed in the program has gotten results. The only thing that has ever stood in the way is commitment — and based on our conversation today, I do not think that is your issue. The question is whether the program is the right fit. Do you feel like it is?"

Section 8

Follow-Up Process

The Rule

Every prospect who does not close on the call gets a follow-up. No exceptions. The goal of follow-up is not to chase — it is to stay present and add value until she is ready.

Post-Call Follow-Up (Same Day)

Send within 2 hours of the call ending.

"Hey [Name], really loved our conversation today. Based on everything you shared about [specific goal she mentioned], I genuinely think this is what you have been looking for. I want to make it easy to take the next step — here is the link to get started: [payment link or next step]. If you have any questions before then, I am right here."

Follow-Up Sequence (If No Response After the Call)

Day 2 — Value add
"I was thinking about what you shared about [specific thing] — and I wanted to send you this. [Relevant testimonial, community win, or insight from the program.]"
Day 5 — Check in
"Hey [Name], wanted to follow up on our call. Is [timeline she mentioned] still where your head is at for getting started?"
Day 10 — Nudge
"Still thinking about you and what we talked about. The spot is here whenever you are ready — just let me know."
Day 20 — Breakup
"Hey [Name], I have reached out a few times and I do not want to keep interrupting your life. If the timing is not right, I completely understand — I will check back in a few months. If anything changes before then, you know where to find me."

⚠️ After the Breakup Message

Move to Nurture in GoHighLevel. Set a 60-day follow-up task.

Onboarding Schedule

Closers: 3–5 days of prep (recordings, framework, roleplay), then a live role-play with Khalil directly. He reviews and clears you to take real calls.

  • Read this handbook in full
  • Review Khalil's call script (linked in the Sales Call Process resource)
  • Watch 2 of Khalil's recorded calls on Fathom before your first day is over

Days 2–5

  • Watch 3 more recorded calls — a curated mix of Khalil's and other closers' (e.g. Jess) so you see more than one selling style, not just founder-style selling. Note the first 5 minutes, objection handling, and closes

Week 3–4

  • Solo calls with weekly recording review
  • Weekly KPI check-in with Khalil via Telegram
  • First independent close attempt (Closers) or first fully owned book from DM to booked call (Setters)
  • Full ramp complete by end of Week 4

  • Every call is recorded on Fathom — no exceptions

  • You own your numbers — close rate, book rate, show rate — all of it
