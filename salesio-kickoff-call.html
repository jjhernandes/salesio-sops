The FoundationStep 01

The Sales.io Closer Model

Everything we teach lives inside three overlapping pillars. A student who's weak in even one of these caps out — walk through where they already have strength, and where the gap is.

Identitywho you are off the call
Psychologywhy people actually buy
Skillthe mechanics of the call
Identity — self-worth, state, and standards off the call show up on the call. This is the one most reps skip.
Psychology — PASE typing, cognitive dissonance, the Heaven & Hell framework. The "why" behind the buy.
Skill — scriptless selling, objection handling, tonality. The mechanics, last, not first.

The CommitmentStep 06

The Sales.io Promise

What we owe each other. Read this together — out loud is better than silent. It sets the frame for the next 12 weeks.

We Must

  • Build you a real, customized gameplan for your first 6 weeks — not a generic curriculum.
  • Coach you every single week, live, so you're never more than 7 days from the answer you need.
  • Hold you to a higher standard than you hold yourself, and call it out when you're playing small.
  • Give you access to the full coaching team — Slack, WhatsApp, weekly group calls, all of it.
  • Keep every coach actively closing deals, not teaching from past glory.
  • Open the job platform to you — 60-80+ vetted companies hiring, updated weekly.
  • Show up for the quarterly in-person mastermind in Orlando, and do the work with you live.
  • Tell you the truth even when it's not what you want to hear. That's the job.

You Must

  • Show up. Mock Call Monday, your 1:1s, the group calls — camera on, present.
  • Be quick to implement and quick to ask for help. Don't sit stuck for a week alone.
  • Tell us if something isn't working. Silence doesn't get fixed — a real conversation does.
  • Keep your commitment for the full 12 weeks. Half-effort gets half-results.
  • Respect the community. Everyone here is your peer, not your prospect.
  • Actually apply what you learn on real calls, not just in the group chat.
