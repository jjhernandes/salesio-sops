How People Actually Decide

The Four Levels
of Communication

Ranked by processing speed. What you say is the slowest signal in the room, and the last one they trust.

01Fastest, most important
How You Feel About Yourself
Pure energy, impulse. Felt the instant you walk into a room, before a word is spoken.
02Fast
Your Intention
The emotional environment. Felt before it's processed, good or bad intentions land before logic catches up.
03Medium
How You Say It
Pattern recognition kicking in, the brain scanning for reassurance: good scenario, bad scenario, safe, not safe.
04Slowest, least important
What You Say
The words themselves. Conceptual, deliberate, the layer everyone trains and the one people trust least.

The Mechanism of Change

Belief, Identity,
Action

First
Belief
What someone has quietly decided is true, about themselves, their options, or what's possible for them.
Then
Identity
The story built on top of the belief. Who they've decided they are, and what they'll defend to stay that person.
Finally
Action
Everything they do, or won't do, flows downstream of the identity, automatically, without a decision being made.

You don't sell the action. You challenge the belief underneath it. Change the belief, the identity shifts on its own, and the action stops being something you have to close them into.
