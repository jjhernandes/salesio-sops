The DM Setter Flow

How to take a girl from first DM to booked call. Four things to uncover, in order: her struggle, her goal, why now, then pitch the call. Not a script to copy and paste. A map, with questions that work.

Opener→1. Struggle→2. Goal→3. Why now→4. Pitch→Book

The rules of the DM: one question per message. Match her length (she sends one line, you send one line). Lowercase, warm, girls' girl energy. React to what she said before you ask the next thing. If she asks "is this gigi?", be honest: you're on gigi's team.
Opener

Start the conversation

Objective: get her talking. Open off whatever brought her in.

"Already doing ugc or just getting started?" is the best first question you have. It tells you which version of the struggle questions to use.

Stage 1

Struggle

Objective: find the real thing stopping her from getting paid. Go deeper on one answer instead of asking five different questions.

If she's just getting started

  • what's been stopping you from starting so far?
  • have you tried reaching out to any brands yet? what happened?
  • do you have any videos or a portfolio yet, or starting from zero?
  • how do you feel about being on camera?

If she's already doing UGC

  • how many paid deals have you landed so far?
  • are brands mostly paying you or just sending free product?
  • how are you finding brands right now?
  • what are you charging per video? how did you land on that?

Go deeper

  • what do you mean by that?
  • how long has that been going on?
  • how has that been making you feel?
i've pitched like 20 brands and nobody answers
ugh that's so frustrating. what are you sending them when you pitch?
just a dm saying i'd love to work with them and my page
got it. and do you have sample videos you send with it or just your page?

UGC struggles to listen for

She saysWhat's really going on
"i don't have enough followers"Biggest myth in UGC. Brands pay for the video, not her audience. Don't correct her in the DM. Note it, the call handles it
"brands only send me free stuff"Stuck in gifted collabs. Doesn't know how to ask for money or what to charge
"nobody responds to my pitches"No portfolio, wrong brands, or a weak pitch
"i'm awkward on camera"Confidence. Very common, very fixable
"i don't know what to charge"Pricing and usage rights. Leaving money on the table
"it seems so saturated"Fear she's too late. Usually means she hasn't started
"i got one deal then nothing"No system for consistent brand deals or retainers
Stage 2

Goal

Objective: get a real monthly number, then the life behind the number. The number tells the closer what to build. The life is what makes her buy.

  • if ugc was working exactly how you want, what would you be making a month?
  • what would that money change for you?
  • are you looking for side income or to fully replace your job?
  • what would your day look like if this was your full-time thing?
honestly like $3-5k a month would change everything
ok i love that. what would $5k a month change for you?
i could quit my job and be home with my daughter
that's such a good reason 🥹 how old is she?
Get to the "why" behind the number. Home with the kids, out of the 9-5, her own money, proving to herself she can. That's what she's actually buying. Log it word for word in GHL for the closer.
Stage 3

Why now

Objective: find out why this can't wait, and check she's serious enough to invest in herself. No urgency = no show and no buy.

  • what made you want to finally make this happen now?
  • what happens if a year from now nothing's changed?
  • how long have you been wanting to do this?
  • on a scale of 1-10, how serious are you about making this work?

Commitment check (ask before you pitch)

  • how much time could you realistically put into this each week?
  • have you ever invested in a course or coaching for yourself before?
  • if you found the right program, are you in a spot to invest in yourself right now?
  • is this a decision you'd make on your own or with your partner?
Partner on the decision? Ask if they can be on the call too. A partner who wasn't on the call is one of the top reasons for "i need to think about it."
Stage 4

Pitch the call

Objective: connect her struggle to her goal, and offer the call as the bridge. You're pitching the call, not the program. Never price in the DM.

Use her exact words. The formula: what you said → what you want → the call gets you there.

ok so from what you're telling me, you've been pitching brands for months with nothing back, and you want to get to $5k a month so you can be home with your daughter
honestly that's exactly what our team helps girls with. would you be open to hopping on a quick call with them? they'll look at where you're at and map out exactly what you'd need to land paid deals consistently
no pressure at all, if it's not a fit they'll tell you straight up

Close it out

Book

Give 2 specific times. Don't just send a link and hope.

amazing! i have [day] at [time] or [day] at [time], which works better? 💕
thursday works!
done ✅ you'll get a confirmation text. there's a quick video in there, watch it before the call, it'll answer most of your questions so the call is all about you

Then: log everything in GHL notes (struggle, goal + why, why now, partner, investment readiness) in her words. The post-booking text and no-show protocol take it from there.

Don't book her if...

  • She's under 18
  • She's clear she can't invest anything right now and won't be able to soon
  • She's just looking for free tips and won't answer the questions
  • She has zero time to put into this

Stay kind, point her to Gigi's free content, and tag her nurture in GHL. A bad booking wastes a closer slot that a ready girl could have had.
