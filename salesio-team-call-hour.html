The Team Call Hour

Sales.io · what actually happens in 60 minutes, closers vs. setters
Closers
0–5
Numbers, first, no discussionCash collected vs. BAM. State it, write it down, move on.
5–25
Call review or role playOne real call, rep self-assesses first — or one live objection, worked as a group.
25–45
Reps on the specific gapWhatever review just exposed, drill it live, right now, not next week.
45–55
Pipeline checkWhat's closing this week, what's stuck, who needs help on a deal.
55–60
One win, outQuick and genuine. End on time, every time.
Setters
0–5
Numbers, first, no discussionQualified sets booked vs. BAM. State it, write it down, move on.
5–25
Set review / cross-referencePull a set recording. Fold in closer feedback on what's actually qualified.
25–45
Live role playDM opener or phone script, reps on the exact objection coming up most.
45–55
Calendar checkBookings for the week, gaps to fill before Friday.
55–60
One win, outQuick and genuine. End on time, every time.
