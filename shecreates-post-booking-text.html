The Post-Booking Text

One text, sent from Gigi's phone the minute someone books off the Thursday webinar. It gets context for the closer, warms the lead up, and gets her to watch the pre-call video so she shows up ready to buy.

Who sends
Setter on Thursday Night Triage
When
Within 5 min of booking, while the webinar is live
From
Gigi's number (via GHL)

Why this works

  • It breaks the pattern. She just watched Gigi for an hour. A text "from Gigi's phone" stops the scroll and gets a reply.
  • It's honest. We say up front it's a teammate, not Gigi. That builds more trust than pretending.
  • The closer walks in prepped. Whatever she shares goes straight into GHL notes before the call.
  • Show rate goes up. Someone who replied and watched the video has already said yes twice before the call starts.

This is the same play running on Mino's Content Academy funnel. Adapted for Gigi and her girls.

Step 1: The opener

Send this as three separate texts, back to back. It reads like a real person typing, not a blast.

Gigi's number
hey {{contact.first_name}}, it's gigi!
well actually it's [your name] using gigi's phone because she's still on the webinar 🙈
i know you just booked in, but wanted to reach out and get some context for the team. really anything you'd want to share with us before your call?
Keep it lowercase and casual. No exclamation-point overload, no emojis beyond one. It should feel like a girl texting from her friend's phone, not a brand.
Only use this if it actually comes from Gigi's number. If GHL sends from a different number, swap line 2 for: "it's [your name] from gigi's team, she's still on the webinar". Never say it's Gigi when it isn't.

Booked outside the webinar? Use this instead

hey {{contact.first_name}}, it's [your name] from gigi's team!
saw you just booked your call, so excited for you. anything you'd want us to know before we chat?

Step 2: Handle the reply

Everything after the opener is manual. Answer like a real person, keep it short, and always end by sending the pre-call video.

"Is this really Gigi?"

wait is this actually gigi??
haha it's her phone but it's me, [your name]! i'm on her team. she's still live on the webinar answering questions rn

"Is this AI?" / "Is this a bot?"

is this AI lol
lol no, real human! i'm [your name], i help gigi with calls. promise i'm not a robot 😂

She shares context

honestly i've wanted to start ugc for months but i have no idea how to get brands to pay me
ok that's literally the #1 thing we hear and it's so fixable. i'll make sure your coach knows that going in

Log it in GHL notes word for word. Her exact words are gold for the closer.

She asks about price or "what's included"

how much is it?
totally fair q! that's exactly what your call is for, they'll walk you through everything and see what fits you. the video below covers a lot of it too

Don't quote price over text. The call is where value gets built first.

No reply after 30 minutes

no worries if you're still catching up on the webinar! here's a quick video to watch before your call, it'll answer 99% of your questions 💕 [post-booking page link]

Step 3: Send the pre-call video

Every conversation ends here, no matter how it went.

love that. before your call, watch this quick video, it'll answer 99% of your questions so we can spend the call on YOU 💕
[post-booking page link]
To confirm with Gigi's team: is there a post-booking page with a short video yet? Content Academy uses a 6-minute one. If She Creates doesn't have one, record it before rolling this out. It's what makes the whole play work.

Step 4: Hand off to the closer

  1. Drop everything she shared into the contact's GHL notes.
  2. Tag the closer in the SCA Internal Discord if there's anything big (spouse needs to be on, money worry, already tried another program).
  3. Note whether she replied and whether she watched the video. This is how we'll track if the play is lifting show rate.
