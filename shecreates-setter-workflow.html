Micah

Your daily phone workflow

01
Morning: confirm today's booked calls
Open GHL → Calendars. Check Ulani's and Kirsty's calendars for today. Every call today should be triaged, have notes, and the closer should have a quick voice note from you on what to expect.
You're on Mountain time. Anyone you can't reach before 9am Eastern, call that afternoon. Confirmation is the most important thing you do.
Touch point timing: booked 3+ days out, triage right away. Morning call, touch point the evening before. Afternoon or evening call, touch point that morning. On the triage call tell them: "I'll give you a quick call the day before to make sure you got the resources."
02
Work the GHL priority lists, in order
P1. New applications. Qualified applicants. Text with the intro snippet, double dial, and if it goes to voicemail leave a 10-second one: "Hey, this is Micah from Gigi's team. I saw you submitted an application, wanted to go over a few things so we can point you in the right direction."
P2. Booked calls. Everything on the calendar that still needs triage or a touch point.
P3. Webinar registrations. On webinar day this jumps to #1. 3-5 minute call: why they signed up, get them excited, make sure they show. You're warming them up, not booking.
P4. Webinar attendees who didn't book. Mikaela takes these (see below).
P5. No-shows and cancellations. Faris handles these. Only touch them if P1 to P3 are done.
Do not dial the orange, non-qualified applications (picked $500 or "not able to invest"). Those wait for the weekend setter.
03
Slack: claim leads so you don't double up
Set #sales-call-booked notifications to all new posts, and star #new-applications.
React on the lead when you work it: 👍 claimed, ✅ picked up, 🔄 rescheduled or call back later.
First come, first served. If one of you reached out and got no reply, the lead is free for the other after 12 hours. Say so in the group when you take it.
04
GHL habits
Add yourself as a follower on every lead you call. Never make yourself the owner, the owner is the closer.
Leave an internal note after every real conversation.
Use snippets: the text intro ("Hey {first name}, this is {you} with Gigi's team, She Creates Academy") and the resource email (who Gigi is, the latest webinar replay, the walkthrough doc). Make your own if they help.
05
Track your numbers
Install the Tally Counter Chrome extension and count: pickups, convos (over 2 minutes), sets, DQs, hangups and follow-ups. Put the totals in your end-of-day report.
There's no 100-dial target right now. There aren't that many leads to call. Work the lists above fully and fast.
Mikaela

Your daily DM workflow

01
Where to look
Check TikTok first, that's where most messages come in. Then Instagram, both Primary and General. When Gigi posts a story, it goes to both.
Open everything. If it turns out to be personal, mark it unread so Gigi sees it.
Expect 4 to 5 conversations at a time after a story post.
02
The flow: conversation → pitch → application → booking
Start from their question and tie it back to the script ("Are you new to UGC?"). Follow the story reply flow below.
After they say yes to the pitch: "Perfect, I just need you to fill out this quick form so we're prepared for the call, then I'll send over the calendar."
The application shows up in #new-applications. Qualified: "Perfect, I just reviewed it, you sound like a great fit for what we do," then send the booking link.
Not qualified on money: "I saw you mentioned you're not able to invest in yourself right now. Was that you being cautious, or is that a literal answer?"
Stuck on a reply? Give Claude the script and the message and ask for a response idea, then make it yours. Questions only Gigi can answer go to her.
03
Post-webinar follow-ups
People who attended but didn't book (GHL list P4). Opener: "I noticed you attended the webinar but didn't book a call. Did something come up, or did you need help with the calendar?"
