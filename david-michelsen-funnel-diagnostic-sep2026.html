Where The Money Actually Leaks

Built off 6 months of real data: the manual DM outreach tracker, discovery-call calendar, and the "First to $5,000" challenge round 1 numbers, checked against the target funnel model. The short version: the problem isn't getting attention, it's getting paid.

Data window: April–September 2026 Prepared for: today's meeting Source: DM trackers, Meta ad spend, Typeform, Google Calendar, cross-checked 5 Sep 2026
The One Thing That Matters Most

Over 6 months: 24 discovery calls booked, ~22 showed (90%), ~21 got pitched (95%). Then 1 actual close with money collected. That's a ~5% close rate against a 30% target, and the note on the data is direct: most non-buyers said yes to the service and stalled on money. Every stage before the close is working. The leak is entirely at "yes" → "paid." Scaling ad spend or DM volume right now feeds more people into a funnel that converts 1 in 21 at the very last step.

05

Diagnosis — Ranked By Actual Leverage

Not the order things happen in the funnel. The order that actually moves revenue.

1

Fix the close-through leak

1 paid close from ~21 pitches over 6 months is the single biggest number in this whole diagnostic. Find out exactly why verbal yeses stall on payment, timing, trust, payment method friction, something else, before touching volume anywhere. Every dollar spent on more leads right now is feeding a funnel that converts 1 in 21 at the final step.

2

Diagnose the August DM dead zone

394 trojans, zero booked calls, despite raised activity. This needs a real answer before DM volume is trusted as a lever going forward — could be message fatigue, targeting drift, or a tracking gap, but it shouldn't be assumed away.

3

Let challenge round 1 finish before re-planning around it

Show rate and CPL are already tracking worse than the target model assumed. Close rate is still a guess. Wait for the real number before rebuilding the monthly math around this channel.

4

Then decide the primary top-of-funnel channel

Ads are already running measurably worse than planned ($82 vs $67 CPL, 29% vs 50% show). Manual DM is extremely time-expensive for its current return (159 DMs per booked call). Don't scale either until priority 1 is fixed — a better channel feeding the same 5% close rate just wastes money faster.

06

Questions To Resolve In Today's Meeting

  1. What's actually happening in the gap between "yes" and "paid"? Get the last 5-10 stalled prospects' real reasons, not a general sense of it.Is it price, payment method, timing, or cold feet after the call ends?
  2. What happened in August? Trace whether it's messaging, targeting, tracking, or attention shifted to the challenge launch.
  3. Is the $6,000 split-pay offer still the 1-on-1 product, and is $5,000 challenge-specific? Confirms whether these are two offers or one offer being tested at two price points.
  4. What's the actual plan once challenge round 1 closes out? Repeat monthly as designed, or adjust based on what round 1 actually shows.
  5. Does David have bandwidth or interest in a real payment-collection process (deposit at verbal yes, immediate invoice, structured follow-up) instead of relying on the call itself to produce cash.
