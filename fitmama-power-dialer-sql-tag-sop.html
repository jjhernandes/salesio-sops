Power Dialer & SQL Tag

Two things to know. That's the whole doc.

1Power Dialer

It rips through your call list automatically so you're not manually dialing one by one.

  1. Log into SendBlue
  2. Open your bucket (your dial list)
  3. Hit dial — it runs down the list on its own
Bucket looks empty or wrong? That's a known syncing hiccup, not you. Flag Khalil, and in the meantime dial manually from the Opportunities tab in GHL using the SendBlue plug-in.

2SQL Tag

Lead came in lower-quality (NQL), but once you actually talked to her, she's genuinely qualified? Tag her.

🏷 SQL
Add this tag on her contact record in GHL

That's the whole action. Add the SQL tag, the automation handles the rest. You don't need to do anything else.

Only tag what's true — this feeds real data back to our ad tracking. Tag loosely and you mess up the numbers for everyone.
