01

The Guarantee

We're confident enough in our process that we're willing to remove the risk of getting started. If you enroll with Social Era Media during today's call, you'll receive our 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

During your first 7 days, our team will:

  1. Conduct your full onboarding call.
  2. Review your business — offer, current marketing, ads, funnel, and existing systems.
  3. Perform a deep analysis of what's currently working, what's not, and where we believe you're losing opportunities.
  4. Walk you through our findings and explain why your current system may not be producing the results you want.
02

The Requirement to Qualify

The only stipulation

You must complete your scheduled onboarding call and onboarding form.

The guarantee is a risk-reversal on doing the work with us, not a no-questions-asked refund on signing up. If the onboarding call and form aren't completed, the guarantee does not apply.

03

Terms

Window
7 calendar days from your initial payment.
To qualify
Scheduled onboarding call completed, and onboarding form submitted.
To request
Notify Social Era Media in writing within the 7-day window.
Refund
Full amount paid to Social Era Media under the agreement.
Availability
Only when enrolled during the sales call on which the guarantee is offered.
Commitment
No long-term commitment required if you exit within the window. No hard feelings.
04

On the Call — Talk Track

For reps to read live when presenting the guarantee. Only attach it when the prospect is enrolling on the current call.

"Whichever option you choose, I'll also give you our 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee. The only thing we ask is that you actually go through onboarding with us. We'll spend about an hour learning your business, you'll complete the onboarding form, and our team will analyze your current marketing and systems. We'll show you exactly where we believe things are breaking down and why. If you go through that process and decide within the first 7 days that you don't want to move forward, you can request your money back. The guarantee is something I can only attach to the offer if we get you started today."

05

Acknowledgment

By signing below, client confirms they understand the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee is contingent on completing the scheduled onboarding call and onboarding form, and that any refund request must be submitted in writing within 7 calendar days of initial payment.
