Strategy

Segment strategy

Never engaged (99)

Never had a real conversation, so a "reactivation" message will read as a cold open, not a follow-up. Goal is a low-friction pattern interrupt, not a pitch.

  • One message, genuinely short, referencing something specific to Sales.io right now
  • No ask beyond a single easy reply
  • If no response, let it rest, don't stack more messages on top of the 1-3 already ignored (see Evan Brown)
Template
"Hey [Name], JJ here. Been a minute since you joined Sales.io, curious where things are at for you right now. What's the biggest thing standing between you and where you want to be in sales?"
Warm (6)

Already mid-conversation. Several are job-board applicants (Roia Roca, stathis Tsigkas sent Looms for the Hiring Directory), not coaching reactivations, don't lump into generic reactivation copy. Reply to what they actually asked for.

Cooling (1–3 months, 18)

Did real work (orientation, week 1, a psych call) and then life happened. Reference the actual thing they engaged with, not a generic nudge.

Template
"Hey [Name], you went through [orientation / week 1 / the psych call] a bit back and then things went quiet. No pressure, just want to know if it's still something you want to pick back up, or if the timing's just not right. Either answer's fine, just want to know."
Cold (3–6 months, 22)

Longest gap, needs the strongest hook. Several completed orientation and never got further, this is where an upsell/re-offer angle fits naturally, as "here's what's different now," not a hard pitch.

Template
"Hey [Name], it's been a while. Sales.io's grown a lot since you were last active, new structure, new coaching cadence. Wanted to reach out directly rather than let this go cold for good. Worth a quick conversation about where you're at now?"
