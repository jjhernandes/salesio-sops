1-on-1 gating logic: tie access to a specific result, not a ticket count — e.g. unlocked once a member hits [specific milestone]. That keeps it scarce and outcome-linked instead of turning into an open support queue, and it doesn't touch the front-end sale at all, so it costs nothing in affiliate conversion.
