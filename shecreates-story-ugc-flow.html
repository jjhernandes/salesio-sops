The "Comment UGC" Story Flow

Gigi posts a story that says "reply UGC." She replies with the keyword, an auto-DM delivers what was promised, and a setter takes it from there into a real sales conversation and a booked call.

Story→ She replies "UGC"→ Auto-DM→ Setter takes over→ Booked

Speed is everything. She replied to a story because she's interested right now. Answer within 5 minutes of her reply to the auto-DM. An hour later she's back to scrolling and the moment is gone.
Step 1

The story

Objective: make a specific promise worth replying for. "Reply UGC" with no reason gets nothing.

  • "want my exact pitch template i used to land my first paid brand deal? reply UGC 💕"
  • "i'm sharing the 5 brands paying beginners right now. reply UGC and i'll send it"
  • "you don't need followers to get paid for ugc. want me to show you how? reply UGC"
  • "saving 10 spots for thursday's free ugc training. reply UGC for the link"

Story replies land straight in the DMs, so this beats "comment below" on a post. One clear CTA per story, keyword in caps.

Step 2

The auto-DM

Objective: deliver what she asked for fast, then ask one easy question that starts the real conversation.

Two messages, back to back:

UGC
yay here it is!! 💕 [link to the thing promised in the story]
quick q so i can send you the right stuff, are you already doing ugc or just getting started?
Always deliver first. She asked for something. Give it to her before you ask anything. It builds trust and makes her want to answer.

Step 4

The sales conversation

Same four objectives as every DM, in order. One question per message, match her length.

ObjectiveQuestions that work
1. Struggle
What's stopping her from getting paid		"what's been stopping you from starting?"
"have you reached out to any brands yet? what happened?"
"are brands paying you or mostly sending free product?"
"what are you charging per video?"
2. Goal
A number + the life behind it		"if ugc was working how you want, what would you be making a month?"
"what would that money change for you?"
"side income or replace your job?"
3. Why now
Urgency + ready to invest		"what made you want to make this happen now?"
"how much time could you put in each week?"
"if you found the right program, are you in a spot to invest in yourself?"
"decision you'd make on your own or with your partner?"
4. Pitch
The call, not the program		"ok so [her struggle], and you want [her goal] so you can [her why]. that's exactly what our team helps with. open to a quick call with them?"
Tie it back to the story. She replied for a reason. Use it: "did the pitch template make sense? have you sent one yet?" is a great way back in if things stall.
